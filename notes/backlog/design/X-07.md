# X-07 — A rotated int8 latent KV cache for Hy4 (`--kv-cache-dtype i8`)

The item (`optimisation-backlog.md`, `#### X-07`) asks for a design of the rotated int8 KV cache Flash-Next uses:
the plugin refuses `--kv-cache-dtype i8` at declare today (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash.cpp:974`), the core already
sizes and moves i8 KV groups, the Hy4 writers and readers do not. This note is the design only: no kernel changes
land with it. §0 is the decisions, §1 what the cache and its ops are at HEAD, §2 the row form (rotation, scales),
§3 where the inverse rotation is folded, §4 the store, §5 the readers, §6 per group, §7 verify == decode bytes, §8
TP, §9 checkpoints / prefix tier / streaming, §10 the memory arithmetic, §11 the KL protocol, §12 the measurement
plan, §13 the risks, §14 the four build cards, §15 what this note saw and left alone.

**One line:** the latent row becomes `[512 int8 codes | 4 f32 scales | 64 bf16 roped-key columns]` = 656 B instead
of 1,152 B (−43 %), each 128-column group rotated by one orthonormal Walsh-Hadamard block before it is quantised;
the inverse rotation is applied once per query row (in the absorbed `q_lat`) and once per output row (in the sink's
store), never per cached token; the indexer's keys, the recurrent tail and the Engram tokens keep their bytes, so
which tokens get selected does not change. At TP2 a 1M-token context costs 8.56 GiB a card instead of 14.46 GiB.

## 0. The decisions

D1. **The switch is the existing global flag.** `--kv-cache-dtype i8` (`core/engine_bringup.cpp:1067-1068` maps
    `i8 → RAD_I8`, `fp8 → RAD_F8E4M3`). Two dtypes only: `bf16` (the default, today's bytes) and `i8`. `fp8` stays
    refused, naming i8 — the wording `docs/GUIDE.md:1131-1134` gives every other model.
D2. **The row is `[L codes | L/128 f32 scales | R bf16]`** = 656 B at L = 512, R = 64. Not the generic
    `rad_kv_row_elems` int8 form (`2*HD+4` = 580 B), which would give the whole 576-column row *one* f16 scale.
D3. **The rotation is block-diagonal and orthonormal:** one 128-point Hadamard transform a block, `R = WHT128/√128`,
    `R^T R = I`, applied to the 512 latent columns of a row. 4 independent blocks a row, one f32 scale a block.
D4. **The codebook is the tree's existing int8 quantiser**, not a new one: scale `amax/127`, round-to-nearest-even
    into `[-127, 127]`, symmetric, a zero block → scale 1 and codes 0 (`libr4d/r4d_quant_act_fp8.hip:59-60`). The one
    difference is that the *stored* scale also carries `1/√128`, because a cache row has no weight scale to fold the
    transform's `√128` into (`r4d_quant_act_fp8.hip:55-58` folds it into the weight at convert; a row cannot).
D5. **The rope part stays bf16, verbatim.** 64 of the row's 576 columns, the position-rotated key, unquantised.
D6. **The inverse rotation is folded twice a row, never per token:** into the query (the absorbed `q_lat`, once a
    (row, head)) and into the *output* (once a (row, head), at the sink's store or `merge_sink`). The staged tiles
    and the cached tokens are only dequantised (§3).
D7. **Nothing is refolded into `W_UK` / `W_UV`.** They are quantised weights; a refold would change the weight bytes,
    the prefill path's math and the weights' own quantiser, for a lever that is about the cache.
D8. **ABI: `RadKVGroupDecl.row_bytes`**, appended, `int64_t`, 0 = today's `rad_kv_row_elems` formula. ABI 45 → 46.
    The group stays a `RAD_I8` group with the honest `head_dim` 288, so the pool, the host tier, the streaming gate,
    the `flex` floor and the loan all keep sizing from one function (`Builder::kv_layer_bytes`, exported as
    `rad_kv_layer_bytes`, `abi/rad_builder.h:511-514`).
D9. **Per group:** the sparse latent and the sliding latent go i8 on the same flag (F-12 and F-13, one lever, one KL);
    the indexer's keys, the indexer's tail, the Engram tokens and the rope columns keep their bytes (§6).
D10. **The readers dequantise to bf16 at the LDS stash**, so the sink's arithmetic order — and therefore the stable
     law and the fold-vs-fused parity — is the bf16 sink's, unchanged (§7).
D11. **TP:** replicated groups, byte-identical rows on every rank; no cross-rank reduction in the store, no
     block-wide reduction for a scale; the existing exact bf16 reduce is untouched (§8).
D12. **Checkpoints, tier, streaming are byte moves** and follow from D8; a persisted prefix file must record the kv
     dtype (§9).
D13. **The gates:** ships opt-in at mean KL ≤ 0.01, top-1 ≥ 97 %, no growth past 200 K tokens; becomes a default only
     at mean KL ≤ 0.001 and top-1 ≥ 99 %, the bar today's generic i8 K/V cache already clears (0.000436 / 99.37 %,
     `docs/GUIDE.md:1119-1121`). Only X-07d proposes a default (CARDS.md §3).
D14. **Four cards, in this order:** X-07a the row form and the store (no served change), X-07b the readers and the
     switch, X-07c the sliding window, X-07d the lossy review and the default (§14).

## 1. What the cache is at HEAD

The plugin declares five KV groups (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash.cpp`); every one of them is bf16.

| group | kind | layers | block | row at HEAD | a token | declared at |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `kv_latent_sparse` | `RAD_KV_FULL` | 12 | 4 tokens | 576 bf16 = 1,152 B | 13,824 B | `latent_group`, `:698-707` |
| `kv_latent_sliding` | `RAD_KV_WINDOW` (512) | 30 | 16 tokens | 576 bf16 = 1,152 B | 34,560 B a token; 400 blocks reserved (8 slots × 50) = 211 MiB | same function, `sliding` |
| `kv_index_keys` | `RAD_KV_FULL`, dense read | 12 | 4 tokens | 32 bf16 = 64 B | 768 B | `:845` |
| `kv_index_tail` | `RAD_KV_LINEAR` | 12 | — | 12 KiB × 8 slots | — | `:827` |
| `kv_engram_tokens` | `RAD_KV_CONV` (i32) | — | 8 | 32 B × 8 | — | `:1147` |

Paged cost 14,592 B a token (`notes/backlog/audit/E-memory-placement-kv.md` §1.6), 0.89 GiB per 65,536 tokens.

`latent_group` is the whole plugin-side declaration of both latent groups: `kind`, `dtype = RAD_BF16`,
`window`, `n_head_kv = 1`, `head_dim = row/2 = 288`. The block size is not declared, it is bound from the first
consumer's `block_size` param (`rad_builder.cpp`, `find_kv_consumer`).

The ops that touch the latent rows:

| `kv_store` | `arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_attn.h:307` | the row copy, `RAD_STR("kv_dtype","bf16")` |

The readers validate `kv.dtype == RAD_BF16` and nothing else (`r4d_mla_sink.hip:664`); the shapes declare the cache
operand `opd(BF16, blocks, 1, bs, L + R)` (`libr4d/r4d_shapes.cpp:872-910`). The staging copies `W/8` uint4 pieces a
row (72 at 576 bf16), one wave-quarter a 128-column block plus the 16 rope columns.

The refusal is the first statement of `declare`:

```
if (ctx->kv_dtype != RAD_DT_INVALID && ctx->kv_dtype != RAD_BF16)
    return refuse("the latent and indexer caches are bf16; run with --kv-cache-dtype bf16");
```

The core side is already dtype-generic: `rad_dtype_bytes` × `rad_kv_row_elems(dtype, head_dim)`
(`abi/rad_builder.h:62-72`: bf16 `2*HD`, i8 `2*HD+4`) in `Builder::kv_layer_bytes`
(`core/build/rad_builder.cpp:478-492`) and `finish_kv_groups` (`:2564-2586`); the host mirror uses the same pitch; the
streamed gate accepts `RAD_I8` (`core/kvstream/bringup.cpp:69`); the prefix cache hashes 4-token entries; the loan,
the reclaim and the `flex` floor are byte arithmetic. What is missing for Hy4 is the row's 656-byte geometry (the core
would size 580) and the plugin's and kernels' i8 forms.

The tree's four precedents, each of which this design takes something from:

- **Generic i8 K/V** (`arch/flint/flint.cpp`, `libr4d/r4d_attn_decode_h256_gqa6.hip:60`): per-(token, head) f16 absmax,
  **no rotation**. Measured mean KL 0.000436, top-1 99.37 % on Qwen3.8-27B (`docs/GUIDE.md:1119-1121`). Hy4's latent
  is one head of 576 columns, so that granularity is 1 scale a row here — not enough; and it is why the rotation is
  the item's point.
- **A mixed row with named sections** (`r4d_attn_decode_h256_gqa6.hip:129`, `O_V4`: E4M3 K, int4 V in groups of 32
  with an f16 scale): the tree's kernels already compute section offsets inside a cache row. A 656-byte row with
  three sections is not a new idea, only a new geometry.
- **GLM's fp8 latent** (`arch/common/rad_block_glm_mla.h`, `kv_dtype`, descale 1): halves the latent with no scale
  plane and no rotation — a 4-bit mantissa over the raw latent, including the rope columns. We pay 80 B a row more
  (scales + bf16 rope) for ~4 more bits and an error that does not follow the row's outliers (§2).
- **The indexer's int8 page** (`RADIANCE_HY4_INDEXER=int8`, `arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_indexer.h:18-22`,
  `libr4d/r4d_dsa.hip:22`): FWHT + a packed u8 page with an f32 scale — the working example of exactly this
  transform in this tree, and proof that the transform costs nothing measurable. It is a *separate* lever; X-07 does
  not fold it in (D9).

The building blocks the row form needs all exist: `libr4d/r4d_fwht.h` (`r4d_fwht_wave<HAD>`, one wave holds a
128-block as 4 contiguous values a lane, five DPP lane exchanges, no LDS, no barrier, bits independent of the lane
mapping), the quantiser convention at `r4d_quant_act_fp8.hip:59-60`, and an epilogue that already does transform +
int8 (`mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g`, `r4d_mla_kv_bf16.hip:272-395`, `r4d_fwht_wave<kQG>` at `:395`).

## 2. The row form: rotation and scales

```
offset   bytes  contents
     0     512  int8 codes, the row's latent columns 0..511 rotated and quantised in four blocks of 128
   512      16  four f32 scales, one a block, in block order
   528     128  64 bf16 columns: the roped key, verbatim
   656           = 41 uint4 pieces, 16-byte aligned at every token
```

- **Rotation:** for each 128-column block `b`, `x_r = WHT128(x_b)`, `s_b = max|x_r| / (127·√128)`,
  `code = rne(x_r / s_b)` clamped to `[-127, 127]`. The stored row's decoded value is `s_b · code ≈ WHT(x_b)/√128
  = R x_b`. One constant is shared by every site that touches the row: `kRowRotScale = 1/√128 =
  0.08838834764831845` (f32), in the store's scale, in the query rotation and in the output un-rotation.
- **Why 128:** `r4d_fwht_wave<128>` is the tree's register shape (4 values a lane, one wave a block, DPP only), the
  quantiser's own block is 128 (`quant_act_fp8.hip:66`, `SUB`), and 512/128 = 4 blocks. A per-block absmax of 128
  rotated values is close to the block's own maximum, so the code's step is ~1/254 of the *block's* maximum, not of
  the row's (which, with one dominating channel, is many times larger).
- **Why a rotation at all:** the latent is rms-normed with a per-channel weight (`w (L)` in
  `mla_row_norm_rope_store`), so the row's channel scales are unequal and a few channels dominate the absmax; a
  rotation spreads one dominating channel over its whole block, so the error is uniform and bounded by the block's
  absmax. It also decorrelates the codes from the fixed weight columns that a per-channel quantiser would round the
  same way every token. A dominating channel confined to one block costs only that block.
- **Why not per row (one scale for 512 columns):** the same argument backwards; a row's 512 latent values span a
  dynamic range a single 8-bit scale cannot carry. Four scales cost 16 B of the 656.
- **Why not fp8 (E4M3) with the same scales:** the bytes would be the same (1 B an element) and the mantissa 4 bits
  instead of 7 + a per-block scale, with a relative error that grows with the distance from the block's maximum. int8
  with an f32 block scale is strictly better at the same size; GLM's descale-1 form (§1) is 576 B a row (no scale
  plane, the rope columns quantised too) and much worse. fp8 stays refused (D1).
- **The rope columns stay bf16:** they are the position-sensitive part, they are not normed, and they are 128 of the
  656 bytes. Rotating them would need the indexer's and the draft's rope arithmetic to follow, for no bytes worth
  having. `kv_index_keys` is separate and stays bf16 (D9).
- **Zero and non-finite blocks:** a block whose rotated values are all ±0 stores scale 1 and codes 0, so a decode
  gives 0 and not NaN — the tree's own quantiser rule (`quant_act_fp8.hip:60`). A *non-finite* value would give an
  infinite absmax, hence an infinite scale and all-zero codes, i.e. a block of NaNs on decode; the store therefore
  tests finiteness once a block (`isfinite(absmax)`, one compare, not one a value) and stores scale 1 / codes 0 for
  such a block, so a poisoned row reads as zeros in that block instead of a NaN page. The fused store's inputs are
  already finite in practice (`rmsnorm` with a finite `eps`, a finite rope), so this is a guard, not a case.
- **Precision, stated:** the decoded row differs from the bf16 row by at most half a code step of its block
  (`absmax_b / 254`), plus the reader's bf16 rounding of `s_b · code` (≤ 2⁻⁹ relative, i.e. bf16's own precision).
  That is the lossy lever, and it is the KL's job (§11), not a tolerance anybody can argue from first principles.
- **Alignment property:** 656 = 16·41 keeps every row 16-byte aligned and the sink's staging whole-piece
  (`kPieces = 41` instead of 72 a row), so no ragged tail, no unaligned uint4.

## 3. Where the inverse rotation is folded

| site | what it does | count at a decode step (M rows) |
|---|---|---|
| the store's i8 form | WHT128 of the 512 latent columns, `1/√128` folded into the stored f32 scale | 4 wave transforms a token-layer row, once, when the row is written |
| the absorb's epilogue (`mla_project_kv`) | `R` on `q_lat`'s 512 columns: WHT then × `kRowRotScale` | 4 a (row, head) = 128 at M = 1 |
| `merge_sink` / the fused sink's store | `R` on the f32 accumulator's 512 columns, after the fold | 4 a (row, head) = 128 at M = 1 |
| the staged tiles, the cached tokens | nothing, they are dequantised only (§5) | 0 |

The score is `R q · R x = q · x` (both rotations orthonormal), and the value is `R (Σ p R x) = Σ p x`, so no factor
is lost and no scale is patched afterwards: the `1/128` of the two nested transforms is exactly the two
`kRowRotScale`s.

**Why not per tile or per token.** The alternative — stage the code rows and rotate each back before the GEMM — is
128·(selected tokens) transforms a step: 98,304 wave transforms at 2048 staged tokens over 12 layers against 128.
The query side is three orders of magnitude cheaper because a query row is written once and read against thousands of
tokens. This is the same choice vLLM's DSA and Flash-Next make (rotate q, not k).

**Why into `q_lat` and not into `W_UK`.** `q_lat` has exactly one writer (`mla_project_kv` absorb, `attn.h:414`) and
one reader family (the four sinks, `attn.h:454/455/477/681`), so rotating it is a local epilogue. Refolding `W_UK`
would change the weight container's bytes, the dense path's operand and the weights' quantiser — a different lever
with its own KL (D7). The absorb's output fragment layout is the one question the card must settle: if its epilogue
holds 4 contiguous columns a wave, the rotation is a `r4d_fwht_wave<128>` inline (the `mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g`
pattern); if not, a one-row op `mla_q_lat_rot` is added at 42 launches a step and its cost is measured. The note
prefers the fused form; the card decides from its own build, and either way the query rotation is one place.

**Why the output un-rotation and not `W_UV`:** the same reason, plus the fold. Both output sites hold the f32
accumulator as 4 contiguous columns a lane over 128 threads = exactly one wave a 128-block, and applying the
transform *after* the fold leaves the fold-vs-fused parity law untouched (§7).

## 4. The store

Three forms at HEAD, each gets an i8 sibling, selected by a parameter — not by a new op name, so no new hook, no new
selection row, and `libr4d_override` + `/admin/kernels/reload` iterate them (`docs/PLUGIN.md` §14):

- `mla_row_norm_rope_store(..., kv_dtype="i8")`: the fused store. Its row is 656 B, its norm and rope are the
  kernel's existing ones, and the new epilogue is per block: 4 contiguous latent columns a thread gives
  `r4d_fwht_wave<128>` (one wave a block), 8 a thread gives `r4d_fwht_part<8,16>` (half a wave a block) — the
  primitive's two shapes for 128, both DPP-only; the card takes the one its loop shape gives, and the two must agree
  bit for bit (a test). A wave absmax (no block-wide reduction, no barrier), one f32 scale, codes with the tree's
  rounding. The rope columns and the norm are unchanged, so **the i8 store's bits depend only on the row's 576 values
  and the parameters — not on the workgroup size, the wave that does the block, or the launch** (the DPP transform's
  stage order is fixed in the primitive). Fallback if the fused loop's mapping cannot be re-shaped at all: write the
  normed row as today, then rotate and quantise from a re-read of it (L2-hot, one extra 1,152 B a token-layer) with a
  wave a block — the card measures which, and the bytes must be the same either way.
- `kv_store(..., kv_dtype="i8")`: the row copy. Same 656 B, same padding rule (`kv_store_body`'s negative slot stores
  nothing, `r4d_model_bf16.hip:828-834`).
- `kv_store_streamed(..., kv_dtype="i8")`: the same bytes into the host mirror at the mirror's pitch (§9).
- The shapes (`r4d_shapes.cpp:980-1030`) accept `RAD_I8` with `shape[3] == row_bytes`, `stride[3] == 1`, 16-byte
  aligned base, and the store's block size from the consumer as today.
- The group declaration (D8): `d.dtype = RAD_I8; d.row_bytes = L + 4*(L/128) + 2*R;` in `latent_group`
  (`hyv4flash.cpp:698-707`), so the pool's block, the mirror's pitch (both `rad_kv_layer_bytes`,
  `abi/rad_builder.h:511-514`), the checkpoint slot and the loan all follow from one function.
- ABI: `RadKVGroupDecl` gains `int64_t row_bytes` (`abi/rad_builder.h:494`); `RAD_ABI_VERSION` 45 → 46;
  `tests/abi_layout_table.h` regenerated from `abi_layout_test`'s printout; `rad_decl_kv_group` refuses a `row_bytes`
  below `rad_kv_row_elems(dtype, head_dim)` or not 16-byte aligned. 0 keeps every existing group's declaration and
  every existing container valid.
- The store's cost at prefill is 4 wave transforms and 128 codes a token-layer row, on a path that already reads
  1,152 B and writes 1,152 B; it is measured (§12), not assumed.

## 5. The readers

- **`mla_attn_paged_sink`, `mla_attn_sel_sink`** take a `kv_dtype` (or read it from the operand's dtype) and keep
  their arithmetic: the staged 656-byte row is dequantised to bf16 into the same LDS stash (`s_b · code`, one
  multiply and one `__bfloat16` a value; the rope's 64 columns are a copy). The score's operand, the softmax, the
  split rule, the row cap and the merge are untouched. `kv_rep` stays 1: the latent is one head.
- **`merge_sink` and the fused sink's store** apply `R` to the f32 accumulator before they write `out`, on 4
  contiguous columns a thread (one wave a block), after the fold.
- **`mla_project_kv` (absorb)** applies `R` to `q_lat` (§3). Its `mode=expand` form and `mla_expand_gate` are
  unchanged: they never see cache bytes, and the expand's `W_UV` multiply is correct precisely because `out` was
  un-rotated before it.
- **The dense chunk path** (`mla_kv_split` + `attn_kv_sink`) is unchanged and keeps reading the chunk's own bf16 rows.
  That is the one place where prefill and decode see different bytes for the same token — the dense path's rows are
  bf16, the cache's are i8 — and it is a stated approximation, not a bug to hide: it sits inside the same lossy lever
  and is covered by the KL (which runs prefill + decode), by the greedy drift and by §7's law (which is about verify
  and decode, both cache readers).
- **The drafter** (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_dfly.h:706-736`) re-declares the same four rows (`kv_store`,
  `kv_store_streamed`, `mla_project_kv`, `mla_attn_sel_sink`) over the trunk's cache; it declares no KV group of its
  own and its rows are the trunk's rows six past the committed end. It therefore takes the same i8 parameters, and
  nothing else about it changes: the rollback, the accepted-prefix trim and the DFly2 acceptance law are over the
  indexer's table and the draft logits, not over cache bytes (`hy4flash_rollback_gate.py` is the check).
- **The indexer kernels** (`r4d_idx_*`, `r4d_dsa.hip`, the pool and the tail) read `kv_index_keys`,
  `kv_index_tail` and `kv_engram_tokens` — none of which X-07 touches — so no indexer kernel changes.
- **libref / libavx: every new form gets a sibling**, as the tree requires (`libref/ref_hy4.cpp:398` for the paged
  sink is the shape to follow): `ref_mla_row_norm_rope_store_i8`, `ref_kv_store_i8`, `ref_mla_attn_paged_sink_i8`,
  `ref_mla_attn_sel_sink_i8`, `ref_mla_project_kv_i8` in `libref/ref_hy4.cpp`; the AVX forms in
  `libavx/avx_hy4.cpp:478`. libref builds the rotation matrix explicitly on the host (a 128×128 multiply, not a
  butterfly) so the reference is independent of the primitive, and the store's codes are compared as codes with the
  half-step bound, not as floats (§12).

## 6. Per group

| group / part | decision | why |
|---|---|---|
| `kv_latent_sparse` (RAD_KV_FULL, 12 layers) | **i8**, 656 B a row | the win: 13,824 → 7,872 B a token; it is the group that makes a 1M context fit (§10) |
| `kv_latent_sliding` (RAD_KV_WINDOW 512, 30 layers) | **i8**, same flag, same row (F-13) | 211 → 120 MiB fixed, and the window's rows are read by the same sink form, so it is the same code; a separate card only because its KL arm and its snapshot bytes are their own measurement |
| the rope part (64 of 576 columns) | **bf16**, verbatim | position-sensitive, not normed, and the indexer's and the draft's rope arithmetic reads it; 128 of 656 bytes |
| `kv_index_keys` (`dense_read`, 12 layers) | **unchanged bf16** | this group decides *which* tokens the sparse attention sees. A quantisation error here moves the selection, which is a different (and much larger) error than a value error. Its own int8 (`RADIANCE_HY4_INDEXER=int8`) is a separate, already-existing lever; hiding it inside X-07 would mix two lossy levers into one KL |
| `kv_index_tail` (LINEAR, 12 KiB × 8) | **unchanged** | a recurrent state carried in f32, not a cache row; nothing to quantise and nothing to gain (96 KiB) |
| `kv_engram_tokens` (CONV, i32) | **unchanged** | token ids, not floats |
| the DFly2 drafter | **follows the trunk** | it shares the trunk's rows and re-declares the same ops; there is nothing to decide beyond passing the dtype, and its acceptance law does not read cache bytes |
| the dense chunk path | **stays bf16** (the chunk's own rows) | §5: a stated prefill-vs-decode approximation inside the same lever |

## 7. verify == decode bytes

The item's law (`kernels/mla_sink_attn.md` E1-E3) says a verify row and a decode row that read the same cache row
must produce the same bytes, and no lever may touch `kv_store`'s bytes except through its own switch. Under i8:

- **The row's bytes are a pure function of the row and its parameters.** The store rotates one wave a 128-block,
  takes a wave absmax (no block-wide sum, no atomics) and writes codes; nothing in it depends on M, the launch, the
  block index or the other rows. A spec row and a decode row therefore see identical 656 bytes — the property the
  law asks for, now with a decode step in the middle of the pipeline.
- **The readers keep the bf16 sink's arithmetic order.** The dequant is at the stash (the load), before any
  accumulation, so the softmax, the split rule, the row cap and the merge are the bf16 sink's; the only difference
  from the bf16 cache is the values themselves (the dequantised row instead of the bf16 row). The split and fused
  forms still fold the same partials → the same bytes, because the un-rotation is applied to the accumulator *after*
  the fold (§3).
- **What is tested, and how:** a byte-level test that does not depend on the model — dequantise a page of i8 rows to
  bf16 on the host, run the bf16 sink over those rows, run the i8 sink over the i8 rows, require the same bytes
  (tolerance 0, any difference is a bug, not a rounding); the same for the sel form; the store's 656 bytes against
  libref's, with the codes compared as codes and the rope columns at 0; and the model-level check that a spec row's
  cache bytes and a decode row's cache bytes for the same token are equal.

## 8. TP

The latent groups are declared replicated (`rad_kv_group_replicated`, `hyv4flash.cpp:1211-1212`): every rank holds a
full copy of the rows.
That is what makes the i8 row TP-safe, and it stays true:

- **Identical bytes on every rank:** the store's transform is wave-local, the absmax is a wave reduction, the
  rounding is the tree's — no cross-rank sum, no rank-dependent order, no atomic. Two ranks storing the same row
  write the same 656 bytes. (A scale per 128-block is already row-local, so a block-wide reduction would be rank-safe
  too; it is a wave reduction because that needs no barrier and no LDS.)
- **The readers' reduce is untouched:** the absorbed attention's output reduce (the exact bf16 reduce the arch
  already does across ranks) sees the same f32 accumulators, already un-rotated, because each rank computes its own
  heads' outputs from its own copy of the rows. Nothing new crosses a rank.
- **TP2 and TP4:** the saving is per card (both/all ranks shrink), so a 1M context is 8.56 GiB a card at TP2 as at
  TP4; TP4's measurement goes to `iks` (`gpu4`), TP2's to the local pair (`gpu2`).
- **What could break it:** a store that reduced the absmax across the workgroup's *other* rows (e.g. a block-wide
  max over 4 tokens) would still be rank-identical but would make the row's bytes depend on its neighbours — refused
  by construction (one wave a block), and tested by the per-row store test.

## 9. Checkpoints, prefix tier, streaming

All three are byte moves and follow from D8 — one geometry, `kv_layer_bytes`:

- **The pool:** a block is `bs × row_bytes` a layer; the sparse block 54 → 30.75 KiB, the sliding 540 → 307.5 KiB.
  The `flex: {kv: {min-tokens: 1048576}}` floor (`docs/GUIDE.md:763-767`) is computed from the same function, so the
  floor becomes affordable without a flag of its own.
- **Checkpoints (in-process, snapshot slots):** a snapshot copies blocks; `RadKVGroupDecl.window` and the linear
  states bound the slot geometry. Hy4 has linear state (the index tail and the Engram conv), so
  `window_ckpt = !has_linear` is false (`core/sched/geometry.cpp:151`) and the window rides in the checkpoint slot:
  64 slots auto, 17.41 MiB a rank (33 blocks × 540 KiB + 96 KiB of state). With 656-byte rows the slot is
  33 × 307.5 KiB + 96 KiB ≈ 10.0 MiB a rank, and the *number* of slots the same host budget buys goes up. A
  snapshot's restore is a byte copy: no re-quantisation, no drift.
- **The prefix host tier and `--kv-resident`:** `kv_store_streamed` writes the mirror at the mirror's pitch
  (`kv_layer_bytes`), `kv_stage`/`kv_resolve` move layer bytes and rewrite block ids — dtype-blind, unchanged. The
  streamed gate already accepts `RAD_I8` (`core/kvstream/bringup.cpp:69`). The i8 store must write the same 656
  bytes into the mirror as into the pool: one form, two destinations.
- **X-01 / X-04 / X-05 interplay:** X-01 (host resident tier) and X-05 (streamed prefix) inherit the smaller pitch
  from `kv_layer_bytes` and need no change; X-04 (a persisted prefix) must record the kv dtype (and, for Hy4, the row
  form's version) in its file header, or a container recorded with i8 rows could be adopted by a bf16 run. That is
  X-04's field to add, listed there as a dependency, not a change in X-07's cards.
- **The prefix cache's key** does not change: one process serves one kv dtype, so a 4-token entry cannot be adopted
  across dtypes.

## 11. The KL protocol

The gate is keptn's KL against the bf16 reference, run on the u3 container with the engine's own KL mode
(`docs/TOOLS.md`, `--kld-record` / `--kld-ref` / `--kld-corpus` / `--kld-out`, keys `kld_record`, `kld_ref`,
`kld_out` in `core/rad_core.h:1187-1202`):

1. **Corpus:** one JSONL of prompts, built from the same prompts and code corpus `scripts/backlog/prompts.py`
   feeds every backlog tool (so both arms, and any lane, score the same tokens), at least 200 K scored tokens so the
   "past 200 000 tokens" column is real. One file, recorded by sha256 and line count in the measurement.
2. **Reference:** the bf16 arm records it (`--kld-record DIR`, `--kv-cache-dtype bf16`), the i8 arm scores against it
   (`--kld-ref DIR --kld-out FILE`, `--kv-cache-dtype i8`). Same container (`hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3.rad`), same TP, same
   `ngram-placement`, same power cap, same build options; only the dtype differs.
3. **Control:** a second bf16 arm scoring against the bf16 recording must come out ~0. It catches a mis-built harness
   before any number is believed.
4. **The numbers:** per-position `KL(P_bf16 ‖ Q_i8)` mean, median, p99, p99.9 and top-1 agreement, plus the same
   restricted to positions past 200 000 (`docs/TOOLS.md:868-960`). Report them for the sparse group alone and for
   sparse + sliding, since they are two cards.
5. **The gates (D13):** opt-in at mean KL ≤ 0.01, top-1 ≥ 97 %, and the past-200 K mean within 2× the first-4 K mean
   (no growth with depth — the property the per-row scale buys, the same one `docs/GUIDE.md:1123` credits the generic
   i8 cache with). Default only at mean KL ≤ 0.001 and top-1 ≥ 99 %.
6. **The rest of the item's Verify** that touches accuracy: greedy text drift and the logprob stats on u3, and the
   DFly2 acceptance chi-square unchanged (`tools/hy4flash_rollback_gate.py`) — the law is the target's for any cache,
   so a change there means the selection moved, which D9 says it must not.
7. **If a gate misses:** the lever stays opt-in with the measured number in `notes/backlog/lossy/X-07.md` (CARDS.md
   §3). The listed follow-ups, in cost order, are a per-64 scale (8 scales a row, 512 + 32 + 128 = 672 B), a fixed
   sign vector per layer (QuaRot's `H·D`, 0 bytes, one XOR), or extending the bf16 tail. None of them is in the four
   cards.

## 13. Risks and what would close the item

1. **The KL misses the default gate.** Likely, and not fatal: the cards are built to ship opt-in (D13), and the
   measurement decides. The 8-bit code with a per-128 scale over a rotated latent is a coarser lever than the generic
   i8 K/V cache, which scales every (token, head, K and V) separately — dozens of scales for a row of a comparable
   size against four here — so a mean KL of 10⁻⁴ is not the expectation; 10⁻³ is.
2. **The store's extra work at prefill.** Four wave transforms + 128 codes a token-layer row on a path that already
   does a norm; measured in X-07a's kbench row before any served run.
3. **The absorb epilogue's fragment layout** (D6/§3): if it cannot hold a 128-block, the rotation becomes a row op at
   42 launches a step. Decision point in X-07b, with the launch cost measured.
4. **The 656-byte row is not the core's `2*HD+4`** (D8): the ABI append is the whole cost, and a container or a plugin
   built against ABI 45 fails to load rather than mis-sizing — the intended failure.
5. **The dense chunk path's bf16 rows** (§5) mean prefill and decode see different bytes for a *new* token's own row
   until it is read back from the cache. It is inside the lever, and §11's KL (prefill + decode) is what covers it.
6. **The `kv_index_keys` decision** (D9) could turn out wrong in the other direction: if the KL is bad *because* the
   selection degrades, the cause is not this lever — the indexer's keys are bf16 here, so a bad KL points at the
   values or the rotation, and the follow-up is a finer scale, not an indexer change.
7. **What closes the item:** the four cards merged, the measurements in `notes/backlog/measurements/X-07b.md` /
   `X-07c.md`, the KL and the default decision in `notes/backlog/lossy/X-07.md`, and the `docs/GUIDE.md` row for Hy4's
   `kv-cache-dtype: i8` saying the row is a rotated int8 latent of 656 B, not the generic `2*HD+4` form.
