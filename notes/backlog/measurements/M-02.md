## 2. The merge bar's three rows

1. **Output, byte-identical: yes.** The four greedy texts hash the same in all twelve runs
   (`d6da4fb33e 0699709c4b e7a23c5d9a cad756fe87` nospec, `5b7a3975de f55fe02071 0075e702df
   9ae27a62e9` DFly2, identical before and after), and the generated lengths match (256/256/102/64 and
   256/256/103/64). A placement policy changes no arithmetic.
2. **The targeted op, faster: no, it is the opposite.** The admission is a copy the LFU path would
   have made once anyway: 32 % of the nospec admissions and 45 % of the DFly2 ones were routed again
   inside their three-step window, and 73 % / 57 % expired unused. Link h2d a `c8` phase goes
   9.53 -> 10.40 GB (median, +9 %) with `nospec` and 11.55 -> 18.28 GB (+58 %) with DFly2; promotions
   3167-3203 -> 3466-3484 and 3786-3918 -> 6105-6198; the slab starves of a pool slot 78-129 -> 137-163
   and 488-542 -> 3319-3755 times a phase. The `step` it adds is a few microseconds of host code; what
   it costs is the copies above.
3. **End to end, not worse: no.** `nospec` c8 median 320.95 -> 320.73 tok/s (-0.1 %, inside noise) but
   its **TTFT 69-71 ms -> 280-281 ms**, four times worse in every after run (the admissions are taken
   while the eight requests are still prefilling); `coupled03.yaml` c8 median **406.20 -> 394.20 tok/s
   (-3.0 %)** with TTFT unchanged at 74-76 ms.

## 3. The lane was shared

iks serves other Moebius runs (the selftest and CI jobs hold a card and its VRAM during part of this
window). Two rows show a neighbour: `m02-a1-nospec` (ttft 5167 ms, 182.4 tok/s) and `m02-a3-dfly`
(wall 5.79 s, 353.5 tok/s). They are left in the table and the medians above carry them; the decision
rests on the counter rows, which no neighbour can move: h2d is higher in **every** after run, and so
are promotions, readmits, starved_pool and quarantined.

## 4. How to reproduce

```
git ls-files -z --cached --others --exclude-standard | tar --null -T - -czf - | \
  ssh iks moebius-gpu/run.sh RadianceHy4Flash-2727-base "<base: eval \"\$RAD_CONFIGURE\"; cmake --build build -j 48; cp build/bin/radiance ../bin-before>"
# then, in the after tree: build, cp build/bin/radiance ../bin-after, and per run
#   cp <arm>/bin / <the after tree>/build/bin/radiance
#   RAD_LANE=iks-A scripts/backlog/run.sh <tag> scripts/backlog/{nospec,coupled03}.yaml c8,greedy
```
