# K-10 measurement: `gemm_nt_f32` with a stored-bf16 weight at verify heights (#31, PR #221)

Measured per CARDS.md §6 step 3, against the merge bar in §2. K-10's own subject, the router GEMM, its
selection and the latent's quantiser in one launch, did not meet the bar and is in `../closed/K-10.md`.
What this file measures is the one part of that change which did: `gemm_nt_f32`'s tile body split into its
sums (`gemm_nt_f32_tile_sums`) and its store. The split changes no arithmetic; it changes what the compiler
makes of the eight-row form (17 to 63 rows) when the weight is stored bf16, which is how the served routers
hold it.

## 4. D-10 and K-52

- K-52's staged prefill form (`gemm_nt_f32_lds_kernel`, 256 / 384 / 768 rows and up) is a kernel of its own and its
  rows are level in both weights (section 1, M 256 and 2048).
- D-10 names "the x-staging of `gemm_nt_f32` at 17-63 rows (33.3 us a layer = 1.4 ms)". Most of that 33.3 us was this:
  the bf16 instantiation of the eight-row form, not the x loads. After this change the form costs 25 us a layer in the
  c8 verify pass and 22-48 us alone at N 512, the same as with an f32 weight, so what staging x in LDS can still take
  off is to be measured against **these** numbers (`--perff32 512 3072 M`, the `rule16` line), not against the
  backlog's. The split leaves the tile's interface as D-10 would want it: the sums are a function, the store is the
  caller's.
