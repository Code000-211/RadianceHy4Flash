# T-03: long-context decode stamps (63K session, c1 / c4), local lane

Measurement-only item: no code arm, so no before / after. The "after" is the probe tooling this PR adds (`probe.py`
phase `lctxN`, record() of composite waves, stamp readers that skip windowless phases); the numbers are three
repeat runs of one tree. Tooling by andymail90-web (PR #186); runs, merge with the base and note by the maintainer.

- Lane: `local` (the Moebius host, 2 x R9700 gfx1201, 210 W read back by `lane.sh`: `cap 210 W` on both cards),
  u3 container, `coupled03.yaml` (DFly2, drafter on), `max-model-len 65536`.
- Commit: `0aef219` (PR head after merging base `6e76ed7`); same build for all three runs.
- Command, three times (`t03-1`, `t03-2`, `t03-3`, each its own server, all `server-exit=0`):
  `scripts/backlog/lane.sh local 'scripts/backlog/run.sh t03-N coupled03.yaml c1,c4,lctx1,lctx4 RADIANCE_AQL_STAMP=1'`
- Context: `lctxN` default `RAD_LCTX_CHARS=190000`; this tree's prompt text tokenises to 62,785-63,890 tokens
  (59,306 for the fourth session), i.e. "60K" here is ~63K, a little above the 58,903 of CARDS.md 1.3 (other prompt
  offset). The decode wave resumed the fill (`ttft` 0.12 s for session 1; 0.9 s for the others at lctx4).
- Stamps read with `agg.py <csv> <json> --match 'idx_score|rowtopk|dfly_sel|idx_pool'` (target full-model pass kind:
  1209 at c1 short context, 1322 at lctx1 long, 1364 / 1322 at lctx4 long).

Findings:

1. **`qsa_select` is stamped as `qsa_local_kernel` + `qsa_merge_kernel`** (correction: an earlier version of this
   note said it leaves no stamp; it was searched for under the op name, but the row `qsa_select_i32` launches as the
   chained split radix select `qsa_local` / `qsa_merge`, 24 launches a selecting pass). From the same three runs'
   `stamps.csv`: 382-390 us a pass on the full selecting pass (pass kind 1322, 0.9-1.0 % of its kernel sum) and
   510-555 us (kind 1364, 0.8 %). That is 0.4-0.6 ms a step against K-25's premise of 1.8-3.7 ms: the premise does
   not hold in a served 63K run, by a factor of 3-9. Also stamped for selection: `dfly_sel_kernel` and `rowtopk`
   (the drafter pass, kind 308, 2.8-4.1 % of that short pass) and `idx_score_kernel` / `idx_pool_gated` (the full pass).
2. **`idx_score` at 63K is small:** 14 us a launch at one decoder (0.17 ms a step, 0.8 % of the pass), 32-57 us a
   launch (0.4-0.7 ms, 1.0-1.3 %) at four, growing with context and sessions but under 2 % of a step. The
   +3.3 ms c1 step growth at 63K is mostly not the indexer.
3. Hit rate falls from 0.975 (c1) to 0.91-0.93 (lctx) and the stage layers count rises (1,312 / 6,399): the 63K sessions
   page KV, which `idx_score` and the mask do not show.
