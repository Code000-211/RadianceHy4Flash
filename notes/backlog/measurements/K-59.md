## 1. What the change adds, and what it refuses

| row | axis | values | read by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mla_project_kv` (absorb, expand), `mla_expand_gate` | `mb` | `{0,1,2,4,8}`, 0 = the law | `kvp_mb` (`r4d_mla_kv_bf16.hip:496`), law `M <= 16 ? 1 : 4` |
| `mla_attn_paged_sink`, `mla_attn_sel_sink` | `hg` | `{0,1,2,4}`, 0 = the law | `r4d_mla_sink.hip:723`, law: the widest 64/32/16-head group that still leaves 64 workgroups |
| `moe_gemm_iqr`, `moe_gemm_iqr_mod4` (gate/up) | `map` | `{0,4,8}`, 0 = the law | `moe_iqr_launch`, `r4d_moe_iqr.hip` |
| `moe_gather_bf16`, `moe_gather_inv_bf16` | `form` | `{0,1,2,3}`, 0 = the law | `moe_gather_body`, `r4d_moe.hip` |
| `moe_gemm_w4a8i8`, `moe_gemm_w4a8i8_mod4` with `w4nl64a8i8h` only | `dmt` | `{0,2,4}`, 0 = the law | `moe_gemm_launch<W4=2, I8>`'s down form (`r4d_moe.hip:2889`, law `T >= 32 * n_expert ? 4 : 2`) |

Every one of them is bit-neutral: the axes choose a block, a group or a form, never a rounding
step. The value that cannot be read is refused, never quietly taken as the law:

- `dmt` on a row or dtype whose launcher does not read it (`moe_gemm_i8a8`, `moe_gemm_i8a8_mod4`,
  and `w4a8i8` / `w4a8i8h` / `w4nl32a8i8h` of the `w4a8i8` rows): `RAD_E_UNSUPPORTED`. Those rows
  are `NOTUNE` now, so rad-tune does not walk them either — this is the review's blocking finding.
- `dmt` on a shape without the wide block (`a_sorted`, one part, `Nc % 256 == 0`), and `dmt` on
  the decode band (`r4d_moe_decode_band(T, n_expert)`), which has no such block: `RAD_E_UNSUPPORTED`.
- `dmt` other than 0/2/4, `hg` other than 0/1/2/4: `RAD_E_UNSUPPORTED`.
- `form` other than 0..3, `map` other than 0/4/8: `RAD_E_INVAL` (a value the row does not name is
  bad input, not an unsupported shape — the same code the plugin uses for a malformed argument).

## 3. The MoE axes at Hy4 prefill shapes

`rad-tune` cannot walk these three axes on this model: no MoE row is in the declared graph of
`hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3.rad` (the engine's MoE rows are declared by the architecture and the tune
graph carries only what the container declares), so they were timed by the plugin's own harnesses
at the same shapes the prefill step runs.

**`dmt`** — `r4d_selftest --case moe_gemm_w4a8i8`, down GEMM `2560 × 384:256`, 512 experts,
top 10, at the routed-row counts of a 2048/4096/8192-token step (×10 rows a token), 3 rounds,
rotated order, `st_time_us` with an untimed warm-up:

| routed rows | token step | `dmt=2` | `dmt=4` | 4 is |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20480 | 2048 | 767.1 µs | 729.0 µs | −5.0 % |
| 40960 | 4096 | 1164.9 µs | 1029.8 µs | −11.6 % |
| 81920 | 8192 | 2477.8 µs | 1988.1 µs | −19.8 % |

The law (`kMoeDownMtRowsPerExpert = 32`) takes the 64-row block from `32 * 512 = 16384` rows, so
at a 2048-token step (20480 rows) it already picks 4 — the shape the item asks about is the one
the law is right on, and the axis' spread is 5 % there and 20 % at the long end.

**`map`** — `r4d_selftest --perfmoeiqr 2048 20 128 8` with `R4D_PERF_IQR_SHAPE=hy4` (a 2048-token
step: 16384 routed rows over 128 experts, longest run 159 rows, 315 run-map entries, 1082 token

The law here is the packed map (`map=8` by rows an expert); `map=4` costs 13 % at the two small
batches and ties at the largest. No change.

**`form`** — `r4d_selftest --case moe_gather_bf16`, at the Hy4 prefill gather (20480 routed rows ×
2560, i.e. a 2048-token step at top 10), prepass included:

|---|---|

| a wave a token (`form=2`) | **347.9** |

The law is the wave form (the 20.6 % faster one) at this shape; the decode shapes the same case
prints (16..1024 routed rows) keep the law at or within 2 % of the best pin.

## 4. The axes the item lists that are not added, with the code that says why

- **`rt`, `kb`** (and `sw`): no such kernel exists in the tree — `grep -rln "rt_kernel\|_kb_kernel\|
  sw_kernel" libr4d/` matches only `r4d_plugin.h`'s declarations and other families' device code,
  not a Hy4 prefill row. There is nothing to select between; they join the table when they land.
- **`gemm_iqr_tiled` tile arms**: `r4d_rad_gemm_iqr_tiled` (and `_m64`/`_dual`/`_gated`) dispatch
  by `iqr_part(kernel_word(b->dtype & kWordMask))` — the weight's part, four fixed arms — not by a
  performance choice (`iqr_by_part`, `libr4d/r4d_gemm_iqr.hip:1513`).
- **`gemm_i8a8_tiled` tile arms**: `r4d_rad_gemm_i8a8_tiled` (`libr4d/r4d_gemm_fp8a8.hip:1409`)
  picks `gemm_8a8_tiled<true, 0, 256>` when the weight's group is `R4D_I8_GROUP_WIDE` and the
  plain form otherwise — again a layout, not a speed, so a tunable over it would be a tunable over
  the weight format.

## 6. The merge bar, and which rule this card takes

CARDS.md §2 asks for byte-identical output *and* the targeted op measurably faster, otherwise
`closed/K-59.md` and no code. Here the output is byte-identical (§5) and **nothing is faster**:
at Hy4 prefill shapes every law K-59 makes tunable already picks the best point of its axis, and
the numbers above are what says so. §2's measurement-only clause — "measurement-only items merge
their note, plus the fix when the measurement calls for it" — is the reading taken: K-59's own
Change line is the axes (a tooling deliverable: the item's Gain is "enables K-41/K-42/K-51
tuning; by itself ?"), its Verify line is "tune report; kbench", and this file is that report.
The measurement calls for the axes to stay: three of the four laws are now *shown* right rather
than assumed, `dmt`'s spread (5 % at a 2048-token step, 20 % at 8192) is on record for K-41/K-42,
and `dmt` on the rows that do not read it is refused instead of silently ignored.

If a reviewer reads §2 the strict way, this card closes with this file and no code; the axes and
their refusals are then `closed/K-59.md`'s content and K-41 re-adds them.

Two more things a reader of this file should not have to look up:

- `moe_gemm_iqr_mod4` carries `map`, and no suite exercises that row at all — neither this one
  (`r4d_selftest --case moe_gemm_iqr_mod4` answers `no smoke case`) nor `tests/r4d_emu/` — at the
  merge base. The axis is read in `moe_iqr_launch`, which `moe_gemm_iqr` runs byte-for-byte at
  `map=4`, `map=8`, `map=0` and `map=3` (refused), and the two rows differ only in the wrapper
  and the class layout. Writing that fixture is not this item's work; the gap is named here so it
  is not mistaken for a hole the axes opened.
- The axis cases in `r4d_selftest.cpp` build their pinned arguments through one helper
  (`args_with`), and `tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_sink.cpp` runs the `hg` sweep above 8 heads only: at
  8 heads the law picks the one group that fits, so the sweep there would launch the same work
  twice and cost this suite time it does not have (120 s).
