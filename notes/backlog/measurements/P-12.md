## What the numbers say, and what they do not

- **Measured:** the lend table above (memory, the item's actual subject), the routed and missed units a step at decode,
  the hit ratio, tokens/s, ms/step, and parity. The routed/missed columns answer what the earlier version of this note
  inferred from the lend line — they are read from the harness, not derived.
- **Not measured:** a throughput difference that survives its own noise. Within one arm `c1` spans 98.33–146.01 tok/s
  (1.48×) and `c8` spans 256.91–281.12; the arm-to-arm medians differ by less than that spread in both phases. The
  closure record's "−0.8 % / +1.5 %" were medians of the numbered runs and should be read as *inside noise*, not as a
  gain or a loss.
- **Not retained:** the boots' `activation arena: … use … MiB` lines that gave the level sizes (91.26 MiB at 64 rows
  against 257.57 MiB at 256). They were read during the run and quoted in `closed/P-12.md`, but the boot logs were not
  kept, so this file does not claim them as raw evidence.
- **Limitation:** the arms ran sequentially, one boot each, not interleaved. That is why no throughput claim is made.

## Why closed rather than merged

The part that pays is the reservation, and its size is real; the part that would make the smaller levels *safe*
(rounding every transient's rows up to the write tile of any kernel that writes it) has nowhere to live — `RadBufDecl`
and `BufferInfo` carry no write-tile metadata. Reopens when the buffer ABI can carry it.
