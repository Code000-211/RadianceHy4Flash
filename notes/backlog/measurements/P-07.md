# P-07 — the Engram junction form saves 16 dispatches a step and costs 25 us a layer

Item: P-07 Junction form for the Engram layers (they fall back to `ihc_pre` + `rmsnorm` + own
quantiser), `notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`.

The junction form is built and lossless: on every run below the four greedy texts are
byte-identical. It is also slower than the launches it replaces, at decode and at prefill, so the
item's merge bar (CARDS.md §2 item 2: the targeted op measurably faster) is not met. The numbers,
and why, are below; the card's note is `notes/backlog/closed/P-07.md`.

## Method

```
export RAD_SRC=<head tree>            # the pf6 prompt is generated from the tree: a fixed corpus
RADIANCE_AQL_STAMP=1 bash scripts/backlog/run.sh <tag> nospec.yaml greedy,c1,pf6
```

for the after arm, and the same line with `RAD_REPO=<head>/build/basesrc bash
build/basesrc/scripts/backlog/run.sh` for the before arm. The two arms are interleaved, run by run,
on the same host in the same quiet windows.

Phases: `greedy` (4 fixed prompts, 256/256/102/64 tokens, the parity row), `c1` (one client, 256
tokens, the decode row), `pf6` (one 7947-token prompt, 2 tokens, the prefill row).

The decode rows are the AQL stamps of the `c1` pass kind (`1079` records before, `1063` after: the
fused form takes 16 more records off the tape). Per-launch times are the median over the passes of
the exclusive time of each launch (`scripts/backlog/layer.py`'s method, computed for every layer in
one pass by `build/p07_layers.py`). Layer windows are between consecutive `moe_iqr` gate_up
launches, so window `L<n>` is trunk layer `n`: the Engram layers are **L10, L20, L30** (and layer 0,
whose window has no left edge) and L11/L31 are plain neighbours of two of them.

The fused junction is bit-identical to `ihc_post` + `engram_gate` + `ihc_pre` + the tail-0 junction:
it calls the same `r4d_eg::row` (`libr4d/r4d_engram_gate.h`) as `engram_gate` does after this
branch's second commit, so the two cannot drift apart.

## Why it is slower

The gate's work is a handful of rows against four weight vectors, tuned as its own kernel: 4 waves,
its own LDS part arrays and a block reduce. Inside the junction it runs at the junction's geometry
(a grid and a wave map sized for the iHC/all-reduce layout), and the ISA suite reports what that
costs: every Engram variant of `ihc_junction_kernel` spills scalar registers (`0 VGPR / 21 SGPR
spills to scratch, 68 bytes per lane`), and the Engram variants of
`ar_ihc_junction_2rank`/`4rank` spill 13–120 SGPRs with 68–228 bytes of scratch a lane. The fused
launch is not 27 us of work in one launch but 52 us: the gate's row alone takes ~43 us there
(52.4 − 5.9 − 3.4) where its own kernel takes 18.0.
