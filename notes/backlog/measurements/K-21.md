## Method

`scripts/backlog/run.sh <tag> <cfg> <phases> [ENV=VALUE...]`, which takes the card reservation
itself and runs on the lane's cards; nothing else of mine touched the cards while a run held them.
Runs of the two arms were interleaved (before, after, before, ...) so that a drift of the machine
lands on both sides.

- **Served step, interleaved**: `coupled03.yaml`, phases `c1,c8,greedy`, tags `b1 a1 b2 a2 b3 a3`,
  3 runs a side, ~1m40s a run; then phases `c8` alone, tags `b4 a4 b5 a5 b6 a6`, 3 runs a side, ~50s
  a run, because the `c8` phase of the first set runs after a `c1` phase and its acceptance is not
  the same in the two settings (the before arm is 6 % faster there, see §1). Raw lines:
  `runs/local/<tag>/run.out` and `<tag>.json` (after), `build-base/src/runs/local/<tag>/...` (before).
- **Op rows, interleaved**: `cprof.yaml`, phases `c1,c8`, `RADIANCE_PROFILE_EVERY=1`, tags
  `pb1 pa1 pb2 pa2 pb3 pa3`, 3 runs a side (a fourth after-side run, `pa4`, was taken for §2's c8
  rows). A run dumps the per-op table every step (cumulative), so consecutive dumps were differenced
  into a step's delta, a step whose delta total is ≥ 200 ms was dropped as a prefill chunk, and the
  report is the median of a round's own calls over the window. The window is the run's first 40 dump
  tables (`c1` runs first) and its last 40 (`c8`); one of the first 40 is a prefill chunk, so each
  window is 39 decode steps and 195 calls a side.
- **Taps**: `coupled03.yaml`, `RADIANCE_HY4_TAPS=1
  RADIANCE_DUMP_BUF=dbg.dfly.cand_idx.4,dbg.dfly.cand_val.4 RADIANCE_LOG=trace`, phase `c1` (tags
  `tb ta ta2`) and phase `c8` (tags `tb8 ta8`, plus `ta8b`, a second run of the after-side tree). The
  probe prints a `hash` line per buffer, step and context; the arms' lines were compared one to one.
- **kbench rows**: `build/bin/rad-kbench --home build/radiance_home --kernels libr4d --ref libr4d
  --timing-only --op row_topk --op cast_row_topk --op row_topk_merge`, one run a tree, on one card
  (`gpu1`).

## 2. The op rows

`cprof.yaml`, `RADIANCE_PROFILE_EVERY=1`, interleaved `pb1 pa1 pb2 pa2 pb3 pa3`. A draft round's own
rows are told from the trunk's output row by op index (`#3000`+); `vocab_off=0` is rank 0's half.

`c1`, the first 39 decode steps (five rows a round, 195 calls a side):

```
before  logits_gemm     5 rows     310.00  311.00  311.00 us/call
        cast_row_topk   5 rows      20.00   20.00   19.00
        row_topk_merge  5 rows       9.00    9.00    9.00
        round                                  339.0  340.0  339.0
after   logits_topk     5 rows     321.00  321.00  321.00
        cast_row_topk   -- not called --      (gone)
        row_topk_merge  5 rows       9.00    9.00    9.00
        round                                  330.0  330.0  330.0
```

A round is **339.3 → 330.0 µs** (the mean of the three runs a side; −9.3 µs, −2.7 %): the f32 plane's
242 KB a row and its `cast` + stage-1 `row_topk` cost 20 µs, the epilogue's top-8 in the head's own
launch costs 10 µs more than the head alone, and stage 2 stays. Five rounds a step make the step
0.047 ms cheaper, which on the served 21.65 ms step is 0.2 % -- the merge bar's kernel gain, inside
the served noise of §1 as the item's own 0.3-0.5 % estimate would have it.

`c8`, the last 39 decode steps (late in the phase, when clients finish and a round is narrower):

```
before  logits_gemm     5 rows     322.00  323.00  321.00   (195 calls)
        cast_row_topk   5 rows      20.00   20.00   20.00   (195 calls)
        row_topk_merge  5 rows       9.00    9.00    9.00   (195 calls)
after   logits_gemm     5 rows     322.00  323.00  321.00   (95 calls, the wide rounds)
        cast_row_topk   5 rows      20.00   20.00   20.00   (95 calls)
        logits_topk     5 rows     322.00  337.50  322.00   (100 calls, the narrow rounds)
        row_topk_merge  5 rows       9.00    9.00    9.00   (195 calls)
```

No row is slower on the after side: the fused row costs the head's own 321-322 µs where the head
alone costs 321-323, and the 20 µs of cast + stage 1 it replaces are gone. `row_topk_merge` is stage
2, the merge across the two ranks' halves, and both arms run it once a round with the same 9 µs.

## 3. The taps

`dbg.dfly.cand_idx.4` and `dbg.dfly.cand_val.4` are the drafter's per-round candidate ids and values
that the fused head writes for stage 2, so they are the change's output:

- `c1`, tags `tb` (before) `ta`, `ta2` (after, twice): 246 `hash` lines a run (123 steps × 2
  buffers), **0 lines differ** in `tb`↔`ta` and in `tb`↔`ta2`, step 0 included.

At `c8` the same dump is **not comparable across runs**, and this is worth saying plainly. Tags `tb8`
(before) and `ta8` (after) both ran the phase with the taps on, and their `hash` lines keyed
`(step, context, buffer)` differ on 368 of 524 common keys -- but so does the *schedule*: `tb8` took
90 decode steps and `ta8` 98, and the eight clients land on different sequence numbers in the two
runs (before, sequence 6 proposes `341 259 5505 11 14491` first; after, sequence 7 proposes exactly
that, and before's sequence 10 line is after's sequence 9's). The two sequences that kept their
number all through the phase, 1 and 2, propose **16 of 16 and 23 of 23 rounds identically**. The
candidate hashes are read per step, and a step's contents depend on which sequences were in the
batch, so a run-to-run comparison at eight clients measures the scheduler, not the kernel.

A third run of the *same* after-side tree (`ta8b`) makes that plain: against `ta8` it differs on 400
of 556 common tap keys, and of its 753 proposal rounds only 39 sit at the same index with the same
tokens, all of them from sequences 1 and 2 -- as much of a difference as `ta8` shows from `tb8`. Two
runs of one build do not agree at `c8`, so the taps there cannot tell a change from a schedule.

What the `c8` rows' shapes need is covered where it is deterministic: `tests/r4d_emu/test_topk.cpp`
compares `logits_topk`'s `idx`, `val` and `pairs` against `logits_gemm` + `cast_row_topk` byte for
byte for `M` = 1 and 2, whole and sharded, `R` = 8, 16, 32, with a tie case, and the served `c8`
phases of the six runs of §1 end with the same greedy texts. Merge bar 1 rests on those and on the
`c1` taps above.

The `row_topk` rows are unchanged (the largest move, M=56, is 1.4 %, inside the 2.2 % spread that

(`kbench` reached 1 of 647 rows with the three prefixes), so they cannot be timed there; §2 times
them on the model instead, where both are 20 and 9 µs a round.

The op that changed is not one of these three rows -- it is the new `logits_topk`, and the cases
come from `build/radiance_home/kernels.rkb`, recorded 2026-11-14 from
`qwen3.8-27b-iq8r-dflash.rad`, a fixture that predates the row, so no case reaches it (the run
reports `1 of 647 declared kernel row(s) ran`). Its cost is what §2 measures, on the model, a round
a step: 321 µs against 311 + 20 = 331.
