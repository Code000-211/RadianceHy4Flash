## What fires on u3

On u3 the drafter's layer linears quantise through `quant_act_fp8`, which is not the rotated int8 form that
`rmsnorm_had_quant_i8g`, `mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g` and `gated_had_quant_i8g` write. Those five families therefore keep
their plain chain, as the PR's guards intend (`quant_act_fp8` is 46 launches a draft pass in both arms). Two parts do fire:

- `mla_row_norm_rope_store` (the latent norm, the key's rotary and `kv_store`);
- the last residual add folded into the final norm (`add_rmsnorm`).

Each of the 5 draft layers drops `rmsnorm`, `rope` and `kv_store` for one `mla_row_front_kernel`. The pass also drops one
`binary` and one `rmsnorm` for one `add_rmsnorm`. Net: **-11 dispatches a draft pass**, 298 -> 287 at c1 and 293 -> 282 at
c8 (the c8 pass is 5 shorter in both arms). At startup the server declares 3096 ops before and 3101 after.

Medians: c1 136.1 -> 137.3 tok/s (+0.9 %), ms/step 22.49 -> 22.17 (-1.4 %). Each after run is faster per step than each

same tokens. The c8 tok/s spread inside one arm (366.6 to 375.9) is as large as the difference: not worse beyond noise.

## Verdict (section 2)

1. Byte-identical greedy output: holds.
2. The targeted ops are faster in ms on the lane: -12 % (c1) and -15 % (c8) of their time in the draft pass, with 11 fewer
   dispatches a pass. Holds.
3. End to end is not worse: c1 +0.9 % tok/s, c8 -3.6 % ms/step. Holds.

Most of D-01's estimate needs the drafter's linears in the rotated int8 form, which u3 does not use. With such a container
the other five families fire without a code change (the unit tests cover that graph).
