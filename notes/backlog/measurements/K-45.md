## 2. Why f32's window is eight slots, not sixteen: the served geometry

Hy4-flash's block does not run the plain gather: `hyv4flash_moe.h:443` fuses it into
`moe_gather_inv_i8g` when the group is 128 or 256 and `top_k == 16` (K-19), so the gather on the
served prefill path is the **quantising** one at 1984 rows x 1536 columns x top_k 16 (31744 routed
rows), group 128, f32 routing weights. `--case moe_gather_inv_i8g` times the fused launch and the
`moe_gather_inv` + `had_quant_act_i8g` pair it replaces, and with the pin held across the timing it
prints each pinned form at that shape too. Three interleaved rounds.

The after library's two timings a round are the unpinned rule and form 3 pinned; its f32 pin 2 is
refused, so it has no third line (`after` and `before`) -- with `47d4c7f9`, which accepted f32 at
this shape, the three lines are the rule, pin 2 and pin 3:

```
  ok    moe_gather_inv_i8g, group 128, n 1536 at M=1984, f32 routing weights: byte-identical to moe_gather_inv + had_quant_act_i8g
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.3 us        <- d61d19c5, round 1
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.4 us  = old
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.2 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.3 us  = old          <- pinned sixteen slots
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.4 us        <- round 2
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.4 us  = old
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.2 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.3 us  = old
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.5 us        <- round 3
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.5 us  = old
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.2 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.3 us  = old
```

`47d4c7f9` (f32 in the wave form at sixteen slots too), one round of three timings -- the rule, the
wave form pinned, the sixteen-slot form pinned:

```
  ok    moe_gather_inv_i8g, group 128, n 1536 at M=1984, f32 routing weights: byte-identical to moe_gather_inv + had_quant_act_i8g
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    315.6 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    289.6 us  = old      <- the rule: the wave form
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    315.6 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    289.8 us  = old      <- pinned wave
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.2 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.2 us  = old      <- pinned sixteen slots
```

(the other two rounds: 315.5 / 289.7, 315.7 / 289.9, 203.2 / 182.2 and 315.0 / 290.0,
315.8 / 289.1, 203.2 / 182.3)

`cf11ed50` at the same shape, two timings a round (its f32 pin 2 is refused:

```
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.4 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.4 us  = old
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, separate    203.2 us
  gather + had_quant_act_i8g g128, fused    182.1 us  = old
  -- round 2: 203.4 / 182.4, 203.1 / 182.2;  round 3: 203.2 / 182.3, 203.1 / 182.0)
```

| at 1984 rows x 1536, top_k 16, group 128, f32 | before `cf11ed50` | `47d4c7f9` | after `d61d19c5` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `moe_gather_inv_i8g` (fused, the served op) | 182.4 / 182.4 / 182.3 us | 289.6 us (rule = wave) | 182.4 / 182.4 / 182.5 us |
| `moe_gather_inv` + `had_quant_act_i8g` | 203.4 / 203.4 / 203.2 us | 315.6 us | 203.3 / 203.4 / 203.5 us |
| the same library's pinned sixteen slots | 182.1 / 182.2 / 182.0 us | 182.2 us | 182.3 / 182.3 / 182.3 us |

The two forms at one geometry, one library, one run: **289.6 us the wave form against 182.2 us the
sixteen-slot form** for the fused op, 315.6 against 203.2 for the pair. Taking the wave form there
therefore costs **+107 us a layer** -- +4.4 ms on a 16384-token chunk over 41 layers -- and it is
the shape and not the dtype: at sixteen slots a row the wave form's slot loop walks twice the
codes. That is why `kGatherWideF32Slots = 8` bounds f32 only: bf16 keeps the window it had, and a
layer whose block routes sixteen slots keeps exactly the kernel `cf11ed50` gave it (182.4 against
182.4 us, same bytes).

## 3. The lane (TTFT, before against after)

The lane ran with the rejected intermediate library, the one that *did* change the served op, so it
is the strict case: `scripts/backlog/run.sh k45-{after,before}-2<i> nospec.yaml pf6,pf20`, the only
difference `libr4d.so` in `$RADIANCE_HOME/kernels/`, five pairs, the host shared with other cards
of this machine:

```
pf6  after 1.999 2.004 2.037 2.107 2.643 s   before 2.030 2.056 2.057 2.234 2.466 s
pf20 after 6.902 7.061 7.284 7.910 8.329 s   before 6.912 6.998 7.196 7.704 8.244 s
```

The sets overlap and the spread inside one arm (0.64 s at pf6, 1.43 s at pf20) is larger than the
difference between the arms: **noise**, which `CARDS.md` §2 allows. With `d61d19c5` the served op
keeps its sixteen-slot form (§2, 182.4 us against 182.4 us, byte-identical output), so that pair is
the worst case and the merged change cannot move the lane at all.

## 4. Which op the lane actually runs (`profile-ops`, stamps)

`profile-ops: true` in a copy of `nospec.yaml` (`k45prof.yaml`), one `pf20` pass each way:

- the served gather is `moe_gather_inv_i8g n=1536 top_k=16 group=128, f32 weights` -- K-19's
  fusion, not the plain `moe_gather` the item's audit row was taken on ("2048-row chunks run the
  2-byte-a-lane `gather_kernel` plus two inverse-table launches").
- the stamps of both arms (`runs/local/k45-{before,after}-21/*.stamps.csv`, 98165 rows each, same
  prompt set; "after" here is the wide-everywhere intermediate, the strict case) hold **exactly the
  same 3772 rows of `r4d_moegather::gather_kernel16_q8<256,true>`** (2.32-123.76 us a launch: the
  fast ones decode steps, the slowest a prefill pass) and no other `r4d_moegather` kernel at all.
  So the served op runs the same kernel the same number of times either way, and cannot be what
  §3's spread comes from.
- no `moe_gather` row and no `gather_wide_kernel` row appears in either run: the lane never runs
  the shape this item speeds up, which is why the gain does not show end to end.

## 6. What the item gets

- The wave form with f32 weights is **-84.8 us a launch** (2.5x) at the shape the item names
  (2048 rows, top_k 8), byte-identical, `LOSSY: no`.
- The served lane's gather is the fused quantising one at top_k 16, where the wave form is **+107
  us a layer slower**; f32 therefore takes the wave form only up to eight slots, and the served op
  keeps the byte-identical kernel it had. The change is additive: no shape can regress, and no
  shape `cf11ed50` served moves.
- The item's audit basis is stale on this model after K-19: the lane runs `moe_gather_inv_i8g` at
  top_k 16, so the item's +0.8 % prefill does not appear end to end. What it can buy end to end is
  the plain-gather prefill path (`RADIANCE_HY4_FUSE_GATHER_Q8=0`) and any f32-routed model whose
  block routes eight slots.
