the item's close: the fused `mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g` at prefill heights against the

## 1. The rows §2 names

`mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g` is the op the item gives a prefill form; the chain it replaces is
`mla_expand_gate` (the gate product) followed by `had_quant_act_i8g` (the quantiser), and the
quantiser's `n` is the product's element count (`heads × v_dim`), so heads=64 pairs with `n=16384`
and heads=32 with `n=8192`. µs, the median of the three runs:

(`after`) — the two trees, two fixtures and six runs agree to 0.2 %, so the comparison above is
between the trees and not between two runs.

## 2. Parity

- `after.rkb`, 88 cases, three libraries against `libref`: **264 checked, 264 passed, 0 failed, 0
  skipped, 0 capped, worst rel_l2 2.745e-04**; 88 cases ran inside guarded allocations, 0 wrote
  outside one.
- `before.rkb`, 296 cases: **888 checked, 888 passed**, the same worst rel_l2, and of its declared
  fusions **136 byte-identical to their declared chain, 0 differ**.
- The fused form's own parity, in `tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_kv.cpp` before the revert: for heads 32 and
  2 and M 65, 70 and 133 the fused emission equals `mla_expand_gate` with `gate=true` followed by
  `had_quant_act_i8g`, **bit for bit**, and its rows equal the same rows run one token tile a
  workgroup.

So the change was lossless; the only question it had to answer was speed, and at every height it is
about 1.45 × the chain it replaces.

## 3. What the numbers say

- The fused launch is not slower because of a bad block: per row it is **0.69 µs** at M=512, heads=64,
  against **1.07 µs** for the one-tile form it extends at M=64 — the MB work made the fused kernel
  35 % faster than its own predecessor, and it is still behind the chain's **0.48 µs** a row.
- The gap is the in-kernel gate-plus-quantiser phase, which costs 2.4–3.3 × the standalone quantiser
  kernel at every height (at M=64: 68.1 − 38.2 = 29.9 µs against 9.1 µs; at M=512, heads=64:
  355.4 − 198.0 = 157.4 µs against 47.4 µs). The traffic it saves — the 16.8 MB gate product written
  and read back, 33.5 MB a layer-step and 1.4 GB over 42 layers — is worth about 2.3 ms of a chunk at
  this card's 600 GB/s, while the slower launch adds 42 × ~110 µs ≈ 4.6 ms.
- Where the fusion does pay is the launch it removes: at decode heights it is worth one launch floor
  (2.3 µs inside a batch, 5.4 µs between two events), and that is K-50 part 1's win, not a prefill one.
