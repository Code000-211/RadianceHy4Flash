# K-50b — the fused gate-quantiser above 64 rows: measurement

Backlog item K-50b (`notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`, issue #266). This is the record behind
the item's close: the fused `mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g` at prefill heights against the
`mla_expand_gate` + `had_quant_act_i8g` chain it would replace, before (the merge base) and after
(the change, which the branch then withdraws).

- **before:** `4744d929` (the merge base of `claude/peaceful-wright-hhqnhi`), built in its own tree
- **after:** `5df83e9d` — the last of the item's four commits on the base, and the tree built and timed
  here: `52ec8e1b` gives `kv_expand_gate_quant` the four-token-tile MB form (`kQMB = 4`), `74877326`
  adds its emu test, `4f914eda` drops the arch's `kFuseRows` cap (the fused op is issued at a step's
  own height, `fused_q = op_expand_quant && abs_rows == T && T > 0`), and `5df83e9d` lifts the row's
  `M` bound from 64 to 65535 so that issue resolves a kernel at prefill heights. The branch reverts
  all four in `fa811e4a` because of the numbers below.
- **lane:** `iks` as a **kernel lane**: `rad-kbench` against a skeleton recorded from the model's own
  declared graph (`sk.rad`, sparse `m.xr`, from `~/models/hy4-flash-iqr-v4/hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3.rad`),
  no `radiance` server started, so no served container and no `ngram-placement` apply
- **measured by:** the card's author (Radiance-Builder, Moebius run 2756)
- **machine:** `iks`, one host — Linux 7.0.14-30-pve, 48 cores, 120 GB RAM,
  4 × Radeon AI PRO R9700 (`gfx1201`, 32 GB), **one card a run** (`gpu1`, TP1 geometry: the model's
  `heads=64` and `heads=32` rows), 210 W cap a card (`hwmon` `power1_cap` reads back 210000000 µW
  against a 300 W default), the report's clock column 2678–3377 MHz over the quoted rows
- **build (both trees, identical):** `~/moebius-gpu/configure.sh`, i.e. `cmake --preset default` with
  `-DRAD_WITH_HIP=ON -DRAD_GPU_TARGETS=gfx1201 -DRAD_HIP_VOPD_REPEATABLE=ON`; host compiler
  AMD clang 23.0.0git (the ROCm SDK's `_rocm_sdk_devel`), device code AMD clang 24.0.0git
  (`d6f6cb691863`) from `~/rocm-repeatable` (ROCm 10.1's libLLVM over the VOPD-pairing patch) — the
  engine's own HIP compiler (CARDS.md §1.1). `cmake --build build -j 48`: 1294 of 1294 targets a side
- **fixtures:** each side records its own declare, so each side's cases are the geometry that side
  resolves, recorded from the same skeleton with `--max-cases 1 --op mla_expand_gate --op
  had_quant_act_i8g`, which holds the rows under test at every height the container declares
  (M 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512; heads=64 and heads=32) and not the whole graph:
  `before.rkb` 74 cases (its fused row at M≤64, where the base fuses too), `after.rkb` 80 cases
  (its fused row at every height; M=512 is the prefill form the item wants)
- **how the times were taken:** `rad-kbench --fixture … --bench --bench-reps 10`, three interleaved
  runs a side in the order before, after, before, after, before, after, each a fresh `gpu1` claim of
  one card; device timestamps around a batch of back-to-back launches after one sustained warm-up
  (the tool's `--bench`, `tools/kbench_timer.h`). Nothing set `ROCR_VISIBLE_DEVICES` by hand.

The chain columns are the before tree. The after tree measured the same two ops in its own fixture,
and it agrees row for row within 2.1 % (most within 0.5 %): 201.71 + 47.71 = 249.42 for the chain at
M=512 heads=64 (0.4 % from 248.52), 101.07 + 24.98 = 126.05 at heads=32 (1.7 % from 123.96), and the
fused row at M=64, where both trees run the same one-tile kernel, reads 68.30 before and 68.60 after
(0.4 %; heads=32 38.24 against 38.20, 0.1 %). So each side's own cases give the same verdict, and
the two fixtures' different case counts do not shift a row.

What the table says:

- The fused emission is **1.39–1.57 × its chain at every height**, above 64 rows as well as below.
  At M=512 heads=64 it is 0.675 µs a row against the chain's 0.485, so 0.19 µs a row behind (39 %
  more). The MB form itself did what it promised: 0.675 µs a row at M=512 is 37 % cheaper than the
  one-tile form it extends (1.072 µs a row at M=64 heads=64).
- The cost is the in-kernel gate-plus-quantiser phase (fused minus the gate product), **2.9–4.1 × the
  standalone quantiser**: 144.8 µs against 47.5 µs at M=512 heads=64, 30.4 against 9.1 at M=64,
  51.8 against 13.2 at M=128 heads=64. The traffic the fusion saves — the 16.8 MB product written and
  read back, 33.5 MB a layer-step, ~1.4 GB over a 42-layer chunk, ~2.3 ms at this card's 600 GB/s —
  is less than the slower launch adds, 42 × 97.3 µs ≈ 4.1 ms a chunk.
- To reach the chain the fused launch would need to lose another 28 % of the M=512 heads=64 time,
  from a form whose remaining cost is work, not overhead. That is the reopen condition in
  `closed/K-50b.md`, and it is why the item closes without code.
