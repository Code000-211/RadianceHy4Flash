## What this settles, and what it does not

- **Settled:** the floor is enough for this lane — the card serves eight clients with the charge at
  17.84 MiB, no OOM, and 142.15 MiB of the old bound is returned to the expert/KV split.
- **Settled:** the fallback path is not exercised on this config: the receipt shows the derived term,
  not the flat-bound label, so the metadata path returned a value for every registered object.
- **Not settled here:** whether one charge a card is right when ROCr scratch is per queue — the review's
  own question. The 3 queues a card this lane runs did not fail at the allocation level, but this
  measurement cannot see a per-queue overrun that stays under the driver's own slack; that remains the
  lane's judgement call, now with the numbers above.
- **Not settled here:** any speed claim. The served rows are parity within noise by design; the item is
  a memory-bound fix, and its bar is the Verify, which holds.
