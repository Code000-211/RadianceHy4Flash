# M-09 measurement: the queue-scratch reserve as a knob (#121)

Measured per CARDS.md §6 step 3 against the merge bar in §2. Code on `m09-scratch-cap`, measured on the house lane.

## Commits

- **Before:** `cf11ed50`, the merge base this branch starts from.
- **After:** `ec3e0a80`, this branch's tip — `core/device/dispatch.cpp`, `rad_dev_resident_scratch_bytes` reading
  `RADIANCE_QUEUE_SCRATCH_MIB` (MiB a CU, clamped 1..64, default 5) instead of a literal `5LL * 1024 * 1024`. With the
  variable unset the arithmetic is identical to before.

## Machine and lane

- House lane: 2 × Radeon AI PRO R9700 (gfx1201, 32 GB), TP2, the engine's own `hy4-u4-tp2-disk.yaml`, cards at 210 W.
- Arms booted in turn on the same tree: default (5 MiB a CU) then 4 MiB a CU. VRAM read from each card's
  `mem_info_vram_used` after load (idle), after a `c1` warm-up, and after `c1`+`pf20`.
- `pf20` is the load that matters: a 25,414-token prompt, i.e. the "larger segment" the +150 MiB figure behind the
  bound comes from.

## Numbers

| | 5 MiB/CU (default) | 4 MiB/CU |
|---|---|---|
| VRAM after load, idle | 33,848,991,744 | 33,801,175,040 (−47.8 MB) |
| VRAM after `c1`+`pf20` | 33,880,457,216 | 33,903,529,984 (+23.1 MB vs default) |
| `pf20` 25,414-token prompt | completed, TTFT 11.260 s, 64.23 tok/s | completed, TTFT 11.037 s, 60.77 tok/s |
| `c1` decode tok/s | 81.06 | 108.26 |

**What this shows:** the reserve is reclaimable — idle VRAM drops by the 32 MiB the charge gives back (47.8 MB measured,
other plan terms included), and after a real workload the 4 MiB arm sits *above* default because the freed margin went
straight to expert residency, which is the point of reclaiming it. The largest-segment case survives either setting: no
allocation failure, no OOM, no restart.

**What this does not show:** a speed win. The `c1` pair (81.06 vs 108.26) is order-biased — the default arm booted first
on a freshly built binary and the arms were not interleaved — and on `pf20`, which ran warm in both arms, 4 MiB/CU is
5.4% *below* default, inside this lane's spread. The item's own case for the reclaim is concurrency at 8 clients (+0.3%
c8), which this pass did not measure.

The default therefore stays 5 MiB a CU: a smaller charge is safe for a workload whose peak has been measured, not in
general, and the knob is how an operator with that measurement spends the margin.
