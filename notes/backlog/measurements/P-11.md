# P-11 measurement: multi-interval liveness in the activation arena

Status: **measured** — the plan (card-less, TP2 at 2048 and 8192 rows, both arms) and the lane (three
interleaved windows, three runs an arm: `c1` for the step rows and `greedy` for output parity, which
came out byte-identical on all six boots). The change gives a buffer its plugin declared
`rad_buf_full_write` one liveness run per stretch of uses instead of the single
`[first_def, last_use]` interval, so the Hy4-flash attention scratch and MoE scratch of one layer take
turns in the same bytes. What that is worth is the arena, and the arena is what the numbers below
are: **1.21 GiB → 908.52 MiB a rank (−330 MiB, −26 %) at the 2048-row plan, and 4.83 GiB → 3.55 GiB a
rank (−1.28 GiB) at 8192 rows**, with the output unchanged and the served step rows inside noise.

(An earlier revision of this note measured `34862f3d` → `2146c092` on `iks` at `max-model-len 65536`;
that is not this head's merge base, so it is replaced by the runs below.)

Read them together: `unshared` is the same 1.39 GiB / 5.57 GiB on both arms, so nothing was added to
or removed from the plan — the same transients now pack in 330 MiB (2048 rows) and 1.28 GiB (8192
rows) less. Before, every buffer had one interval, so peak live *was* the arena and the waste was the
packer's rounding (1.75 KiB); after, peak live sits below the arena by exactly the slack the coverage
guard's bounding intervals allow (8.00 MiB at 2048 rows, 32.00 MiB at 8192), which is far inside what
the runs freed. The printed `GiB` lines carry 0.01 GiB (≈10 MiB) of rounding; the deltas above are
computed from both arms' printed values, so each is good to about ±5 MiB, and the item's claim
("about 0.3 GiB a rank") holds either way.

The dump also names what the leftover waste is: `10 transient buffer(s), 76.60 MiB, had no declared use and
are assumed live for the whole program -- that is the arena's waste, and it goes away when ops name
their buffer operands` (305.63 MiB of such buffers at 8192 rows). Those are the only interval-less
buffers left; everything the change could split, it split.

TP4 plan dumps were not taken: this machine presents two cards, and the fake-rank host backend is
chosen only when no device is visible at all. What TP4 rests on here is the declare, which the
card-free arch tests bind at 1, 2 and 4 ranks
(`a_sizing_declare_at_four_ranks_matches_…`, `hyv4flash_arch_test`) and the new
`the_attention_scratch_and_the_moe_scratch_take_turns`.

## What the numbers say

- **The item's number is the arena, and it is there:** −330 MiB a rank at the 2048-row plan (−26 %),
  −1.28 GiB at 8192 rows, with the plan's unshared transients untouched (1.39 / 5.57 GiB on both
  arms) — the same buffers, packed over their runs instead of over the whole step. `peak_live` is now
  a number of its own beside the arena (900.24 MiB vs 908.52 MiB), which is the log the item asked
  for.
- **The targeted step is where it was:** `ms_step` median 13.250 → 13.219 ms (−0.03 ms, −0.2 %),
  `wall` 3.455 → 3.450 s, `agg_tps` 74.09 → 74.21 tok/s (+0.2 %), `ttft` 76 → 77–83 ms. The six runs
  overlap: the base's spread is 13.187–13.274 ms and the after's 13.201–13.300, and `base1` (13.187)
  is faster than every after run while `base3` (13.274) is slower than two of them. That is what
  noise looks like on this machine, and the direction is not one way. The issue-path guard the review
  flagged — the per-run write-coverage check `Ctx::issue` runs on every operand of a `full_write`
  buffer that has more than one run — is inside these same c1 runs, and it does not show.
- **End to end is not worse beyond noise:** 74.09 → 74.21 tok/s aggregate on one client, inside the
  73.77–74.43 spread of the six runs themselves; one window of three cannot resolve less than that
  on a shared machine.
- **Limits:** TP2 only on the cards (see the plan section on TP4), and the machine was shared with
  other runs of the house rig (load average 20–30 during the windows), which is why the arms are
  interleaved run for run rather than batched.
