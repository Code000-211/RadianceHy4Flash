# C-07 — the context pass this drops is not empty: measured, no gain, withdrawn

The item (issue #95) asks to drop DFly2's **empty** context pass, which costs two barrier rounds
(~5–20 us a step). The export and its gate were built and passed their tests; the lane measurement
below says the pass our config runs is not empty, so dropping it buys nothing and costs a little.

## What it says

The pass the head drops is not empty here: it carries `mla_row_front` and two `add_rmsnorm`s, and the
head does its store with `r4dchain_kv_store` instead. So the work moves rather than disappears — 58.0
us of pass kernels out, 36.7 us of store in, and the draft pass as a whole ends up 29 us (0.58 %)
slower at c8 and 84 us (2.12 %) slower at c1. End-to-end is parity: c8 −1.1 % median, c1 +5.9 % median,
both inside this box's spread.

The call site is what makes it fire: `arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_dfly.h:962` calls
`rad_drafter_no_context_pass(b)` for every dfly2 build, where the item's premise is a drafter whose
pass *issues nothing* (`hyv4flash.cpp:1113-1119`, `draft_pass < 0`). A dfly2 drafter with a cache has
work in that pass, so the export must be gated on that condition, not declared unconditionally.

## Verdict

CARDS.md §2's second clause is not met — the step the item names is not faster; it is gone, and its
cost reappears elsewhere. The card closes with `notes/backlog/closed/C-07.md` and the code is withdrawn
from this PR. Reopen when a block drafter with no cache of its own exists to declare it (then the premise
holds), or when the call is gated on `draft_pass < 0` and the rows above are re-measured.

Raw rows, if they are wanted: the run is `/tmp/c07-out` on the rig, produced by `/tmp/c07-measure.sh`
(base and head trees built with `-DRAD_GPU_TARGETS=gfx1201`); this note carries every number it
produced that bears on the item.
