- `ar_ihc_junction_2rank` (the PCIe junctions): before 1482.1 us (all 1427.7-1714.5), after 1446.4 us (all 1417.7-1500.3): -0.036 ms a step; b4 is the load; b3 is 1427.7 us.
- the whole stamped pass: before 24767 us, after 24805 us (+0.04 ms, inside the run-to-run noise).

The paced counters show the mechanism. Before, the pacing takes every promotion a phase has, on the first mark that
finds room (only 4-5 a phase wait for the flush, late 4-5). After, a mark's byte share (150 us x 27 GB/s =
4,050,000 B) leaves about one copy a step to the flush (late 267-269: 269 over 255 steps), and the copies ride the
attention stretch instead of the routed down. c1 is paced the same way and is small enough that little waits (a1:
paced units 600, marks 579, late 4).
