# M-18 measurement: per-layer Engram placement and step-start async prefetch (#305)

Measured per CARDS.md §6 step 3 against the merge bar in §2. Two parts, both off by default: part 1 measures the
synchronous per-layer placement knob (`--ngram-placement-layers`), part 2 the step-start ids + io_uring prefetch
overlap on top of it (`RADIANCE_HY4_ENGRAM_PREFETCH=1`). The same numbers are posted as the `## Measurement` comment
on #305.

single-stream gathers hide behind GPU queue depth; the real exposure is host-worker gather contention under
concurrency (c8) and prefill.
