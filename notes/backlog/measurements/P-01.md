## 1. Stamped decode pass, c1 and c8 (AQL stamps, TP 4)

Each stamped session ran a c1 phase (one request, 256 tokens) and a c8 phase (8 × 256). Card 0's stamps were cut to the
phase's window and to its most common decode tape (1204 records at c1, 1246 at c8; 255 and 253 passes).
`stamplayer.py` (the exclusive-time method of `scripts/backlog/layer.py`) takes, per launch, the median over passes of
its exclusive time (end minus the later of its start and the previous launch's end). One MoE layer's window runs from
just after layer *L*'s routed gate_up (`moe_iqr_kernel`) to layer *L+1*'s routed gate_up, so it holds one shared arm.
`scripts/backlog/layer.py` itself, run on A1 and B1, gives the same layer-6 totals to 0.1 us (179.4 / 174.8 at c1,
328.6 / 319.6 at c8).

**Where the shared arm sits.** At decode the K-23 fold serves the rows, so the arm is two launches: the fold (the shared
gate_up with the gated epilogue: `iqr_gemv_kernel` at c1, `iqr_m16_kernel` at c8) and the shared down
(`gemm_i8_kpar_kernel<2u>`). At TP 4 every launch in the layer is chained (`ch 1.00`, `ord 0.00`) in both arms; only
`gather_kernel16_q8`, `copy16`, `rope_kernel` and `mla_sink_rows` are ordered, the same in A and B.

- **Before (A):** routed gate_up → `gated_quant` → `moe_down` → `moe_gather` → `copy16` → `latent_out`
  (`gemm_i8_kpar_kernel<4u>`) → **fold** → **down** → the all-reduce junction. The arm is issued after `latent_out`.
- **After (B):** at c1, routed gate_up → **fold** → `gated_quant` → `moe_down` → **down** → `moe_gather` → `copy16`
  → `latent_out` → junction. At c8 (8 rows, past `kSharedFirstRows`), the fold is issued behind the router's
  `quant_act_fp8`, just before the routed gate_up, and the down again sits between `moe_down` and `moe_gather`.
  Both launches now sit beside the routed GEMMs, before `moe_gather`, not after `latent_out`.
