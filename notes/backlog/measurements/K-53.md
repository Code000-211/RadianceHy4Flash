# K-53 — SwiGLU in the routed gate_up prefill epilogue: measurement

## What was compared

- **before**: `cf11ed506bfdb9f2e53228f9849f4e7d61747abd` (merge base): the routed gate_up GEMM
  writes the `[T, 2F]` bf16 plane, then `swiglu_clamped_had_quant_i8g` reads it and writes the
  `[T, F]` int8 codes and `[T, F/128]` scales.
- **after**: `fb2262af` (`moebius/run-2310`, this item's code): one `moe_gemm_iqr_gated` launch —
  the same GEMM with the clamped SwiGLU and the rotated int8 quantiser in its epilogue. The plane
  is neither written nor read.
- Correctness of the after side: byte-identical codes and scales against the pair, checked on the
  GPU by `tests`' selftest case `moe_gemm_iqr` (below). Tolerance: **exact equality of every code
  and every scale word** — the fused epilogue does the pair's own arithmetic (`bf16x2_rne` for the
  gate/up product, `q8_had_group_i8g` for the rotated quantiser, `limit` 10 and 0.25), so anything
  but equality would be a bug, not a rounding difference. Zero and dropped rows are codes 0 at
  scale 1.0 on both sides.

  it is unaffected by which card that was.

## Op-level measurement: the pair against the fused row, same card, same build

`gpu1 build/bin/r4d_selftest build/radiance_home/kernels/libr4d.so --case moe_gemm_iqr` on the
after tree (commit `fb2262af` plus the timing print this note adds). Both paths run on the same
tensors, warm, 20 launches, one stream; the pair's number is its two launches
(`moe_gemm_iqr` + `swiglu_clamped_had_quant_i8g`), the fused number is one `moe_gemm_iqr_gated`.
The byte compare over the same tensors passes in every one of these cases.

- **The item's own shape** (2048 tokens a rank at `top_k` 16 = 32768 routed rows, the model's
  1536-wide expert and hidden size): the pair 4524.0 us, the fused row 4347.8 us — **176.2 us and
  3.9 % off a layer**, which is the plane's 100 MB round trip plus the quantiser's launch.
  The item's expectation (`+1.5-2 %` of prefill) is met with room.
- **Small shapes** (T = 68 rows): the fused row is **3 us a layer slower** (+11 % of a 27 us op).
  At that height the epilogue's serialisation after the tile's GEMM costs more than the launch it
  saves. Prefill is what the item changes; the decode side is a small loss, not a gain, and is
  left as measured here rather than hidden. At 41 layers it is ~0.12 ms a token against a decode
  step of tens of ms.

## kbench: why the served checkpoint has no fused row

`gpu1 build/bin/rad-kbench -m $RAD_MODELS/hy4-flash-iqr-v4/hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3.rad \
--home build/radiance_home --op moe_gemm_q --op swiglu_clamped --bench --max-tok 8192 \
--report /tmp/<side>.md` on both trees (after: `gpu1: cards 3`, before: `gpu1: cards 2`):

- The architecture plugin does take the fusion: the after report declares `moe_gemm_q_gated`
  (5 mentions) and the pair is gone from its graph (`swiglu_clamped*` 8 mentions against 62 on the
  before tree).
- The fused row's cases are then **all skipped**, 56 of them, with

  ```
  moe_gemm_q_gated  (moe_gemm_iqr_gated cannot lay out the table 'w' from trellis:codes=u3[1x1],
  scale=f32[1x*],trellis=i8{65536x1},trellis.mad=u32{2x1},scale.col=f32[*x128]/fwht128:
  container format error) | 56
  ```

  `container format error` is `RAD_E_FORMAT`, and it comes from the shared relayout check
  (`libr4d/r4d_iqr_layout.cpp`, the `mad_value` consistency test of a `trellis.mad` table), which
  the gated row reaches through the very hook the pair uses (`r4d_rad_layout_iqr`, wired at
  `libr4d/r4d_iqr_layout.cpp:1040`). This item's diff adds only that hook instantiation, not the
  check, so the refusal is in shared code that no `moe_gemm_iqr` case reaches on this container
  (the container's `moe_gemm_q` cases are its `w4nl64` down projection, served by
  `moe_gemm_w4a8i8`). It is reported here rather than worked around: kbench could not time the
  fused row on the served checkpoint, so the number above is the selftest's.
- The pair's own rows are unchanged between the two trees, as they must be — same container,
  same geometry, `moe_gemm_w4a8i8` (not the IQR family) — e.g. `M=8192` 7428.62 us and `M=128`
  1067.62 us on both sides, and `swiglu_clamped_had_quant_i8g` at `n=1536 M=8192` 151.21 us
  (libr4d, card 2) against 12891.62 us (libavx, host, 48 threads).
