# P-16 follow-up (#295): the load-aware rule's online row price, measured

`draft-depth-load-from: 1` priced a verify row from the step wall time, so a slow drift of the
step time (the expert pool warming, a disk-placed Engram table) read as a row price. Under the rule
the rows follow the price, so the error fed itself. The fix (commit `eb873a61`) reads each step
against its own local level. This file has the diagnosis, the before/after on the issue's two kinds
of lane, and the output parity.

## 1. Diagnosis (lane A, base tree)

One server per arm, phases `c1,c1,c1,think`. The log arms ran with `RADIANCE_DEBUG_CONC_LOG`,
which writes the tables after each refresh and each step's chain.

| arm | warm c1 (#3) | think |
|---|---|---|
| key | 123.0 tok/s, 24.1 ms, 3.28 t/s, 48 miss | 88.7 |
| no key | 130.3, 21.6 ms, 3.05 t/s, 34 miss | 98.4 |

- **Tokens a step are the same either way. The key arm's step is slower**, with more misses:
  the rule verified *more*, not less.
- The `K` lines at one sequence: the row price went 1.159 (the static prior) -> 0.234 -> 0.050
  (the clamp) by the third refresh. An offline replay of the logged steps through the header puts
  the raw `theta[1]` at 0.04, -0.50, -0.95, -1.10, with `theta[0]` at 35 ms. The threshold
  `lam * row` was 0.005, so the rule verified all 5 drafts on 90 % of steps (no-key stop rule:
  35 %). Mean accepted a verify was 1.98 against the stop rule's 1.96.
- The reason: over the first ~250 steps the step time fell from 40 to 24 ms as the pool warmed.
  The first refresh lowered the price, the rule drafted deeper, and R climbed from 4.7 to 6 while
  the time fell. The regression (memory ~2048 steps) read that as a negative row price. Once R was
  a constant 6, nothing could move it back.
- #288 measured the mirror image on its rig: a dear row and a truncated prefix (3.24 -> 2.59
  tokens a step).
- With the fix, the same lane and phases: row 1.6-2.0 ms, threshold about 0.2. Depth shares
  2:25 % 3:24 % 4:12 % 5:39 %. Warm c1 136.2 tok/s, think 100.2.
