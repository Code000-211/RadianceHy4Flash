## The 12 divergences

All in `kernels_hy4.rkb`, all one shape contract: `qsa_tail_store`'s `state` is `bf16[T,1,4,128]` in the recording and
`bf16[8,1,4,128]` on replay, `qsa_work`'s `nc` is `i32[T]` against `i32[8]`. The gate reports them and does not fail on
them: which shape the contract should pin is the kernel owner's call, not a fixture's.
