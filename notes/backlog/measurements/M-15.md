# M-15: the KV loan counters on /stats, local lane

The code arm of M-15 is the counters (`KVManager::spare_split()` and the `/stats` `kv_loan` object); the compaction is
not in this change (see `../closed/M-15.md` for the holes reading on iks-A and why the compaction is its own card).
The merge bar for a counters-only change: greedy output byte-identical, decode tok/s not worse beyond noise, and the
counters visible on a card. Measured by the maintainer when merging PR #177.

- Lane: `local` (the inference host, 2 x R9700 gfx1201, `cap 210 W` read back by `lane.sh` on both cards), u3,
  `scripts/backlog/nospec.yaml` (TP2, no drafter, prefix-cache 16384 MiB).
- Before: `00c83819` (the base). After: `c6f7469b` (PR head, the base merged in). Same host, same lane, same config.
- Command, each arm in its own tree: `scripts/backlog/lane.sh local 'scripts/backlog/run.sh m15ab-<arm><n> nospec.yaml c1,c8,greedy,idle'`
- Order: b1 a1 (b2: the server did not start, `out of memory` at startup while the cards were shared; not a result)
  a2 b3 a3 b4. Three valid runs per arm, interleaved.

## The item's case on this lane: `long8` with the pool keeping the blocks (one run, after arm)

`scripts/backlog/lane.sh local 'RAD_PREFIX_CACHE_MIB=0 RAD_LONG8_CHARS=140000 scripts/backlog/run.sh m15l8-1 nospec.yaml long8'`
at `c6f7469b`: eight conversations of 42,949-49,616 tokens at once, idle, a decode-only demand, the same eight back
(`host_on false`, rendered `prefix-cache-mib: 0`). Startup: `the pool backs 100% of the worst case (8 sequences all at
65536 tokens)`. One run, a cross-check of `../closed/M-15.md`, not an A/B.

`resume_hit_tokens` 365,504 (every prompt resumed from the cache, TTFT 0.24-0.56 s).

Reading: on this lane the same shape leaves **under 8 MiB of holes**, against 517-972 MiB on iks-A. The difference is
the carve: here the pool backs 100 % of the worst case and nothing is evicted, so the lowest-id-first free list packs
the eight conversations densely; on iks-A (58 % of the worst case: 8 x 46,720 tokens = 93,440 blocks against a 74,248-block carve) the pool fills, entries are
evicted from under the top and the holes appear. So fragmentation is a property of a pool under eviction pressure,
not of long context as such, and the 5.1 GiB of KV the eight sessions hold costs the experts 4,895 VRAM units here
through occupancy (`held`), not holes. A compaction is worth its card only for constrained pools like iks-A's; it is
not part of this change.
