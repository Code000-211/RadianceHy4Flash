## Why verify heights

Speculative decoding (DFly2 here) verifies several drafted tokens a sequence, so a verify step at a few sequences runs
the router GEMM at 17–63 rows. Hy4's router (`glm_router.weight`, N 512 K 3072, bf16 weight) runs replicated below
`GlmTp::kSplitRows` (256 rows), so its verify pass is N 512 at every TP width, with x's rows contiguous; from 256 rows
(prefill) it is column-split (N 256 a rank at TP 2, N 128 at TP 4). The change sends 17–63 rows to the staged form at
N ≥ 512 and at N 256–511 from 32 rows, except 57–63 rows staged 4 bytes at a time, which keep 8x1. Every other shape
keeps its form, so rows at N 128, below 32 rows at N 256, at 16 or 64 rows and the served row at 57–63 rows are
controls.

## Order (interleaved)

Every row set ran in interleaved sessions (A = before, B = after), A1 B1 B2 A2 A3 B3 B4 A4, so that drift does not
favour one side.
- **Round 2 crossover** (B = `cc05324e`), card 0: `rad-kbench` at `--max-tok` 48, 52, 56, 60, 63.
- **Round 2** (B = `803ffed7`), card 0: `rad-kbench` at `--max-tok` 17, 24, 32, 40, 48, 52, 56, 57, 60, 63,
  `--perff32` at N 256 and 512 × M 32, 48, 56, 57, 63, `--case gemm_nt_f32_bf16`; then a TP 4 e2e server for greedy
  parity in every session.
- **Round 3** (B = `803ffed7`, the `a2e748a9` build), TP 4, every session: a profile-ops server (probe `c1,c8`), then an
  e2e server (greedy parity, then three probe rounds of `c1,c8`). 4 profile runs and 12 e2e runs a side.

## 1. The crossover (round 2, before vs `cc05324e`)

Round 1 had the served router row at 63 rows +2.6 % (every B run slower than every A run). Both forms take x in groups
of 8 rows, so 57–63 rows are one more group than 49–56. Staged 4 bytes at a time, the staged form is 11–12 % faster at
48, 52 and 56 rows in every run, and from 60 rows it is no faster: its runs split between a few percent faster and up
to 3 % slower than every 8x1 run (medians +0.5 % at 60 rows, +0.3 % at 63). Staged 2 bytes at a time (`--perff32`,
pitch K+5) it stays faster at 57 and 63 rows in every run (section 3). So the bound is on the 4-byte staging only,
above 56 rows (7 groups of 8).
