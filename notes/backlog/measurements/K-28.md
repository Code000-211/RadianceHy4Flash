## Why the rows are AQL stamps and not `rad-kbench`

At this revision `libref` cannot be loaded beside `libr4d` (or `libavx`): `libr4d/r4d_rows.cpp:206` declares
`attn_dense`'s `max_seqlen` with role `RAD_PROLE_CAPACITY` (T-01, `30c0bba2`) and `libref/ref_registry.cpp:3481` with
`P_INTO`, so the loader refuses `libref` and `rad-kbench` has no reference to check a row against. The conflict is
between two files this PR does not touch and it is present in both arms. The item's own basis is the stamps ("M:
`copy2d16` 4 x 13.6 us + `embed_lookup` 23.4 us"), so the rows below are the same ones: the per-kernel AQL stamps of
the pass head, `scripts/backlog/agg.py --match 'embed_lookup|copy2d16|hc_enter'` over the `c1` window.
