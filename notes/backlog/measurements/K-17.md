# K-17 (full-card cliff check for 1024-thread launches): the step gains the 1024-thread quantiser 8.8-26.1 % at 32 heads and 1.1-5.6 % at 64; the 128-thread expand pays nothing

Item as written (backlog K-17, `notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`): "Expand launches sit on the full-card cliff
wave count at 32 heads: 10-minute check. `r4d_mla_kv_bf16.hip:530` grid (2, 32, ceil(M/16)) x 1024 threads = 2048
waves; rule `r4d_common.h:349-375` (waves in 2017..2048 of a multiple of 2048 run ~5x slower; measured on 256-thread
workgroups); no expand launcher calls `r4d_cliff_rows`. Also on the count: absorb 32 heads M 17..32, `merge_sink`
sliding M = 16 / sparse M = 32, sel_sink 16 slots M = 32." Why: "the stamps do **not** show a 5x expand in sliding
layers ... so the cliff probably does not bite at 1024-thread workgroups; ... A kbench A/B (one padded idle workgroup)
settles it." Verify: "`rad-kbench --timing-only` heads 32, M 1 / 16 / 32, with and without the pad." BI, effort S.

The measured answer, up front: the item's geometry note was half right — the *plain* expand does not sit on the cliff
at all (`mla_project_kv`/`mla_expand_gate` run 128-thread workgroups, `kvp_threads`), and the 1024-thread launch of
this file is the **quantiser**, which *does* pay the window: stepping its heads axis through the window
(`r4d_cliff_rows`, the merged change) gains **8.8-26.1 % at 32 heads** and **1.1-5.6 % at 64** on the measured rows,
with one negative row, M = 16 at 32 heads (**-2.5 %**, same 2048-wave grid as the winning M 1-8 rows but a full
16-token tile). The 128-thread grids — expand, expand_gate, absorb — measure **0.998-1.001** padded or not and are
deliberately left unstepped; a step there buys nothing measured. Bytes are unchanged (the card-less
`tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_kv.cpp` quantiser cases drive the real padded launcher; on the card the kbench check pass ran
108/108 bytes identical on the padded build, first A/B below).

## Who, what and where the round-2 A/B ran

Measured by `Athabasco`, 2026-11-25, on the two-card lane box this repo's clone lives on: 2x **AMD Radeon AI PRO
R9700** (gfx1201; device 0 ran the sweep, device 1 idle; the box's Ryzen iGPU is not a measurement device), power cap
210 W on both cards (`power1_cap` 210000000), ROCm 10.1.0, kernel 7.3.5-arch1-1 (Arch Linux). The runs are host
processes, not container processes: `stilldeadcode/radiance:2.2.0` (`radiance-compose/tp2-quality.yaml`) was taken
down for the window and back up after it, and plays no part in the runs. `ngram-placement`: none of these rows reads
an n-gram table (they are per-kernel `rad-kbench` cases with drawn operands, not a served model), so the placement
carries no meaning here and is named only because CARDS.md §6 asks; the serving stack sets none (the `auto` default).
What was *not* controlled: the cards' clock ramp beyond one discarded warm-up session, and the sustained sweeps trip
the 210 W cap — kbench's own `throttled (PPT0)` retry notices appear in both arms' rows (the appendix shows them
collapsed to one notice each), which is why arms are interleaved and per-case medians, not session sums, are compared.

- **before (base)**: `6e76ed76` (the PR's merge base at measurement time), libr4d built with
  `-DRAD_WITH_HIP=ON -DRAD_GPU_TARGETS=gfx1201`, `libr4d.so` sha256 `4f138768...`.
- **after (head)**: `a0323b02` = `6e76ed76` merged with `d453f57f` (the K-17 code), same flags, `libr4d.so` sha256

## What this says

- **Exposure to the window is the kernel's, not the wave count's alone.** The rule's 5x numbers were measured with
  256-thread workgroups. The 128-thread expand/expand_gate/absorb grids sit at 0.998-1.001 padded or not (round 2,
  24 shapes, identical code in both arms), and the 1024-thread quantiser at the very same counts gains up to 26 % by
  stepping. So `r4d_cliff_rows` cannot be spread by counting waves: each launcher's exposure has to be measured. The
  item guessed the cliff "probably does not bite at 1024-thread workgroups" — it does not bite the expand shapes it
  named, and it bites the quantiser that shares the file.
- **The quantiser's gain is real and the merged pad is only this half.** At 32 heads — every sliding layer's decode
  shape, fused `RADIANCE_HY4_FUSE_EXPAND_QUANT` path — the step gains 2.2 us at M = 1, 7.3 us at M = 32 (round-2
  medians above); times ~30 sliding layers, that is hundredths of a ms per token at batch 1 and a tenth at M = 32.
  The step is the existing `r4d_cliff_rows`; the extra head's workgroup returns at the top of `kvq_body`, and the
  chained wrapper still runs `r4d_chain_done()` for it, which the engine expects since it counts blocks from the
  issued packet grid (`core/device/aql.cpp`, `blocks_of(p)` feeding `chain_issued`). Bytes are identical (check
  arm, above). 64-head models ride the same launcher and gain 1.1-5.6 %.
- **The chained padded block is safe and confirmed against the protocol.** `r4d_chain_wait()` waits for the queue's
  completion counter to reach the record's `target`, and `target` is `chain_issued` at issue time (`chain_record(s,
  slot, chain, s->chain_issued)`, `core/device/aql.cpp:1532` and the overlap path's `:1676`) — the block count of
  *every* chained launch issued before it, each one added as `chain_issued += blocks_of(p)` (`:1556`, `:1712`) from
  the dispatch packet's grid, so the padded head's block is part of every later `target`. A padded block skipping
  the wait adds nothing but its own done, and it reads no operand before it (it returns at the top of `kvq_body`),
  so there is nothing for its missing acquire to guard; a successor cannot pass its wait until every real block's
  done has landed, because its `target` counts those blocks too. Confirmed against `libr4d/r4d_chain.h` and
  `core/device/aql.cpp` (`chain_record`, `blocks_of`). No change needed.
- **A card-less test of the padded head exists and runs in the fast suite.** The quantiser is not in the emulator as
  a driven op, but nothing keeps it out: `r4d_mla_kv_bf16.hip` is an emulator source and the quantiser launcher needs
  no engine record. `tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_kv.cpp` now drives `r4d_rad_mla_expand_gate_had_quant_i8g` at heads 32
  (the padded grid, 33 heads launched) for M 1 / 16 / 32: every row's codes, scales and gated output are bit-equal
  to a run of the same rows alone and of a mid-draw slice, sit next to libref's within the suite's usual bound, and
  the guard tails around the code, scale and output operands stay untouched (a padded head that wrote anywhere
  trips them); the chained form and the no-optional-output form run clean. `r4d_cliff_rows` is asserted on the
  launcher's own grid shapes — 32 heads steps to 33 at every multiple the token tiles hit (zt 1..4) and counts clear
  of the window stay. The whole file passes 182/182 in 2.4 s. What stays card-only is the chain traffic itself: the
  emulator carries no engine record, so wait/done are inert there (`r4d_chain.h`), and the done-per-issued-block
  property is the card's kbench check pass and the stamps'.
- **The audit's T-1 companions are expected nulls.** `merge_sink` sliding M = 16 and `sel_sink` 16 slots M = 32 ride
  128-thread workgroups — the shape measured at 0.998-1.001 above — and absorb at 32 heads rode the same code at
  0.999-1.001. None is worth a padded launch; if anyone re-tests one, the finding here is the prior.
