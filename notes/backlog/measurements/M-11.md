The window lender carves 120 slots (172.03 MiB) a rank, after the KV cache's 5076; the offer is clamped to that and
to one window step, so the gain is +129 MiB = +90 units (0.43 points of the 20992) with at most two live sequences
and +86 MiB = +60 units at three and four. That is inside the item's 73-129 units at 1-2 live sequences and under it
at 3-4 (the item's 211 MiB is the whole window pool; the carve leaves 172 MiB of it to the window lender and a live
sequence's own window takes its blocks back). Hit rate and tok/s move inside noise, the expected size for +0.3-0.4 %
more resident units. The same +129.0 / +86.0 MiB and +90 / +59 units were seen on iks-A by the author.

Merge bar (CARDS.md §2): output byte-identical; the targeted quantity (VRAM lent to the experts, units resident)
grows measurably and deterministically; end-to-end tok/s not worse beyond noise.
