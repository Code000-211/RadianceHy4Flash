item adds recording cases to `data/kernels_hy4.rkb` and rules for how a case is cloned to the item's
heights, so its bar is parity -- the recording still agrees with the reference, card-less and on a card

## The heights, and what each trunk row's clone takes from M

`t04_clone` moves shape[0] of every operand and, for the rows that have one, the row axis its
`T04_ROW_AXIS` entry names. Two rows take more than that from M, because their operands are not all M
tall; each now has a rule of its own (`T04_M_RULE`) that sizes them the way the launch at that height
is sized:

| op | the M 24 / 40 / 48 clone | where that derivation comes from |
|---|---|---|
| every other `TRUNK` row | shape[0], plus the row axis of `T04_ROW_AXIS` | the op's own operand list |
| `kv_store` | scatter list `[M]`; cache `[ceil(M / block_size) + 2, 1, block_size, 576]`, slot table `[M]` with `idx_max = blocks * block_size` | `ref_kv_store_shape` (`libref/ref_shapes.cpp`) |
| `qsa_work` | `work = ceil(M / ratio) + seqs`, stage `[work * ratio, n]`, page and position lists `[work]`; the per-sequence operands `cu [S + 1]`, `state [S, 1, ring, n]`, `sidx [S, 1]`, `bt [S, 2]`, `nc [S]` at `S = min(seqs, M)` | `ref_qsa_work_shape`, and `work` as the arch computes it for a graph declared at this height (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_common.h`: `ceil(T / ix_pool) + max_seqs`) |

At the item's heights that is: `kv_store` cache 8 / 12 / 14 blocks at block size 4 and 4 / 5 / 5 at
block size 16 (so the slot table's `idx_max` is 32 / 48 / 56 and 64 / 80 / 80); `qsa_work` `work` 14 /
18 / 20 with `seqs = 8`, and `S = 8` at all three -- the second round's fixture had 4 blocks at every
height and `work = 136` at all three.

## What this record does not claim

- **No speed claim.** The item names no ms to beat: it adds `rad-kbench` cases and how a case is sized
  at the item's heights, and changes no kernel, op or step. Its bar is the parity above, plus the new
  rows existing, being checked and being timed, which they are.
- **The routed MoE rows are not in `TRUNK`.** They are D-10's largest costs (`moe_iqr` gate_up,
  `moe_down_dec`, `gather_kernel`), and none of them gets a case at M 24 / 40 / 48 here. `TRUNK` lists
  the ops `kernels_hy4.rkb` already holds, `kernels_moe.rkb` is the Qwen 256-expert fixture, and the
  Hy4 routed arm needs a container recording of its own; the recipe's comment above `TRUNK` says so.
  Whether the item's "every trunk row" reaches them is a person's call.
- **The q_a case is the container's i8a8h row, not q_a's own u5-trellis row.** No fixture carries a
  u5 encoding (K-06), and the container files the trunk's dense planes as i8a8h, so the case reaches
  `gemm_i8a8_tiled` at N 1024 / K 3072 / M 2048 -- the pair's shape, on the family the shipped
  container serves.
- **The `qsa_work` cases at M 1 / 2 / 4 stay diverged, outside the item's heights.** Their `nc` is
  recorded as `i32[1]`, `i32[2]` and `i32[4]` (the reference's `min(seqs, M)`) while
  `qsa_work_bf16` declares `i32[8]`, so the report counts them among the 15 divergences on both sides.
  They came with the merge base's fixture, the item's heights are 24 / 40 / 48, and this round left
  them as they are.
- **"Real `qsa_select` tables" are the identity table over the 512 pages of the cache**, as the second
  round's record said: `width >= range` holds for every recorded sel case, so every row is drawn as
  0..511 and the subset-selection branches are reached by unit tests only. Whether that counts as a
  real selection table is a person's call, not this record's.
