# T-04 measurement: `rad-kbench` cases at the Hy4 TP2 shapes that were missing (#111)

Measured per CARDS.md §6 step 3, against the merge bar in §2. No kernel, op or step changes here: the
item adds recording cases to `data/kernels_hy4.rkb` and one rule in how a case's table of pages is
drawn, so its bar is parity -- the recording still agrees with the reference, card-less and on a card

## What this record does not claim

- **No speed claim.** The item names no ms to beat: it adds `rad-kbench` cases and how their timing
  input is drawn, and changes no kernel, op or step. Its bar is the parity above, plus the new rows
  existing and being timed, which they are.
- **Not every trunk row, deliberately.** D-10's largest costs are the routed MoE rows (`moe_iqr`
  gate_up, `moe_down_dec`, `gather_kernel`), and none of them gets a case at M 24 / 40 / 48 here.
  `TRUNK` lists the ops `kernels_hy4.rkb` already holds, `kernels_moe.rkb` is the Qwen 256-expert
  fixture, and the Hy4 routed arm needs a container recording of its own; the recipe's comment above
  `TRUNK` says so. It is a person's call, and the review's third finding says as much.
- **The q_a case is the container's i8a8h row, not q_a's own u5-trellis row.** No fixture carries a
  u5 encoding (K-06), and the container files the trunk's dense planes as i8a8h, so the case reaches
  `gemm_i8a8_tiled` at N 1024 / K 3072 / M 2048 -- the pair's shape, on the family the shipped
  container serves.
- **The 9 stale cases** are named above: recorded, timed, listed under Not covered, and not checked.
