# D-12: the DFly2 `dfly.confidence` head as the verify depth's survival estimate. Closed: it adds under 1 % at c8

**The finding.** The checkpoint's confidence head works as a survival estimate when its input is the
corrected chain state `zc_k`. Prefix-survival AUC is 0.888 against 0.882 for today's
`prod max Q_i`, and the head is better calibrated. It ranks drafts almost exactly as the shortlist
mass already does, though: correlation +0.70 with `max Q_j`, and a worse conditional AUC (0.848 vs
0.862). The load-aware rule recalibrates its statistic online, so calibration alone buys nothing.

An offline replay of the block depth rule on the measured lanes puts the best use of the head
(`prod max Q_i × prod c_i`) at **+0.9 % (lane A) and +0.7 % (lane B) tok/ms at c8** over `max Q`
alone, and +0.3 / +0.6 % at c4. The bar was 2 %. The head alone is level (+0.1 / −0.1 %). A
depth oracle would gain about 10 %, so the head reaches less than a tenth of the headroom. The
step-2 wiring (a per-library op and a readback slot) is not worth it at this size. No code is merged.

## What was run

- **Tree:** `8faf751e`, with #295's online row-price fit already in, plus a local-only diagnostic
  (`RADIANCE_DEBUG_DFLY_CONF`, not committed):
  - two PERSIST bf16 planes copy every round's `z[k+1]` (`chain_hidden`, behind the gather) and
    `zc_k` (`corrected`, behind each round's residual add);
  - in `block_depth_decide`, after the walk's state event, rank 0 reads them and computes
    `sigmoid(w · h + b)` in f64 on the host. The weight is `dfly.confidence.weight` (bf16, 3072
    values), and the bias (f32) is 0.008423;
  - the `RADIANCE_DEBUG_CONC_LOG` S line's chain field gains `/c_z(1..D)/c_zc(1..D)` beside
    `max Q_i`.
- **Exactness of both inputs:** `z[k+1]` reads only the committed text and the anchor. `zc_k` reads
  `x_{k-1}` (an ancestor) through the correction. Neither reads `x_k`, so either would have kept the

- **Lanes:** TP2 on R9700 cards 0 and 1, 270 W, `draft-coupling on`, `draft-verify traversal`, 5

  - **A:** `hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u4`, expert-tiered, Engram n-gram on disk.
  - **B:** `hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u3`, Engram in RAM.
- **Two runs a lane:**
  - **full depth** (`draft-depth-stop 0`, load rule off): every draft is verified, so every

  - **as configured** (`draft-depth adaptive`, stop 0.3, load on from 1): labels censored at the

- **Label:** draft j survives iff the next verify accepted ≥ j drafts.

- **The input is `zc_k`** (the open question of `kernels/dfly2.md` §8.3). On `zc_k` the head is

## Replay (sizing)

- **Steps replayed:** the full-depth run's pure-decode steps, grouped by n.
- **Rule:** `draft_depth_select`'s greedy. Drafts 1 and 2 always stay (`load_min`). Level j takes
  the rows still in at j − 1, sorted by falling p, and keeps the best prefix of `sum p − lam · Δcost`.
- **p:** P(a ≥ j | feature decile, j), from a table fitted on the other half of the steps (2-fold
  by step parity).
- **Cost:** `a_n + b_n R + tiles` (16 / 32 / 48 rows), fitted per n on the lane's as-configured
  run (lane A c8: 54.6 ms + 1.33 ms a row + 3.9 ms past 32; lane B c8: 30.2 ms + 0.56 ms a row).
- **Realised tokens:** `sum min(a, D) + 1`. Each policy is swept over 40 values of lam, and its best
  tok/ms is compared with the `max Q` policy's best.

| lane, n | steps | `prod max Q` tok/ms | `c_z` | `c_zc` | `prod max Q × prod c_zc` | oracle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, c1 | 275 | 0.1101 | −0.64 % | −0.53 % | +0.18 % | +7.7 % |
| A, c4 | 139 | 0.2086 | +0.04 % | +0.20 % | +0.34 % | +4.6 % |
| A, c8 | 260 | 0.2485 | −0.76 % | +0.10 % | **+0.89 %** | +10.1 % |
| B, c1 | 277 | 0.1525 | +0.05 % | −0.09 % | +0.31 % | +6.7 % |
| B, c4 | 143 | 0.3725 | −1.08 % | +0.18 % | +0.55 % | +9.5 % |
| B, c8 | 262 | 0.5210 | −1.13 % | −0.08 % | **+0.74 %** | +9.8 % |

- **The blend does not move with its weights.** `prod Q^a × prod c_zc^b` over (a, b) in {(1, ¼),
  (1, ½), (1, 1), (1, 2), (½, 1)} gives +0.33 … +0.89 % (lane A c8) and +0.40 … +0.74 % (lane B c8).
- **Steps at n = 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7:** about 20 each on either lane, too few to read.
- **The paper's claim:** AngelSpec D-cut reports +15.7 % at high concurrency. Its baseline is a
  fixed depth. Against fixed depth, today's rule already makes +11 % (lane A c8: 0.2230 → 0.2485
  tok/ms) and +7 % (lane B c8). The head adds under 1 % on top.

## Why the head adds little

- **It sees what Q sees.** `zc_k` feeds the lm_head whose top-8 law is Q_k, so a linear probe of
  `zc_k` and Q_k's top mass measure the same thing.
- **The head is per token, and the rule needs a prefix.** `prod c_i` treats the positions as
  independent. `prod max Q_i` does too, but the online tables already correct it by position.
- **What remains is the target's side.** The oracle's ~10 % headroom comes from whether the target
  agrees, and no draft-side quantity known before the verify can see that (the PL rule forbids
  reading it).

## If someone revisits it

- **The probe:** the diagnostic patch is two copies in `hyv4flash_dfly.h` and a host dot in
  `engine_probe.cpp`, about 100 lines, and needs no new kernel.
- **A served version** would need, per library, a `[n, E] · [E]` op behind each round's residual
  add, plus a float in the walk's state readback (`RadMtpCoupleState` has a free `pad_` word).
- **It would pay only** with a rule that is short of draft-side ranking. At these lanes it is not.
