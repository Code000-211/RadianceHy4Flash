# M-15 (KV loan reaches only above the highest held block): MEASURED — 0.5-1.0 GiB of holes sit under the top, the counters are in, the compaction is its own card

Item as written (`optimisation-backlog.md` §M-15, audit `audit/E-memory-placement-kv.md` E-K3): `core/mem/kv.h:487-491`;
M (longctx 4 x 60K mid-run): 5.43 GiB used of 6.98 GiB not lent; add `/stats kv held_top`, `buffer_blocks`; relocate the
highest cached-only entries downward (d2d + prefix remap) above a 256 MiB gap. Effort S (measure) / M; gain up to 1.5
GiB (1,100 units) in the measured case.

The mechanism, at HEAD: the loan is the region above the highest held block of a group, so a free block under that top
is unlendable. `spare_tokens()` (`core/mem/kv.cpp:959-965`) is `min` over the lendable groups of `(n_blocks - held_top -
buffer_blocks) * block_size`, and the arbiter offers it to the expert slab (`core/engine.cpp:2400-2430`). Held blocks
scattered up to a high top therefore pin VRAM the slab cannot have, however many free blocks lie beneath them.

## What landed: the two counters, piece by piece

`KVManager::spare_split()` (`core/mem/kv.h:505-517`, `core/mem/kv.cpp:966-990`) returns the whole split for the group
that binds the `min`: `group, block_tokens, block_bytes, n_blocks, held, held_top, buffer_blocks, live, spare_tokens,
loan_tokens`, where `held_top` is the bucket-rounded top (the exact per-block top would be a 74k-entry walk at 100 ms),
so `held_top - held >= 0` and `live - held_top - buffer_blocks` is the headroom left unlent. `spare_tokens()` is now
`spare_split().spare_tokens` after the same peak decay, so the arbiter's number does not move. It reaches `/stats` as a
nested `kv_loan` object (`core/sched/metrics.h:20-40`, `core/sched/mem_iface.h:122-140` and `:473`, filled at
`core/sched/scheduler.cpp:4412`, mapped in `core/server/adapters.h` `bridge_metrics`, emitted at
`core/server/dashboard.cpp:342-358`), absent when no group is lendable, because the pieces only read together with that
group's `block_bytes`.

Tests: `tests/mem_test.cpp` `the_spare_a_loan_reaches_is_reported_piece_by_piece` (a lender with a held block near the
top says what it holds, where its top is, and what its buffer keeps), `tests/dashboard_test.cpp`
`stats_says_where_the_kv_loan_stops_and_what_keeps_it_there` and `the_bridge_names_the_group_the_kv_loan_stops_in`.
`sched_test`, `mem_test` and `dashboard_test` pass (the dashboard test's two pre-existing `requests.lora` /
`session.lora` checks fail, unrelated: the page reads two fields the route does not emit, in files this change does not
touch).

- The loan is at its ceiling in every view (`headroom_mib 0.0`): what the pool withholds is holes, not an unlent
  reserve.
- 9,812 free blocks under the top = 517 MiB after the batch has finished and is idle, 18,439 = 972 MiB once the eight
  conversations come back and lift the top to 74,119 of 74,248. At 100 MiB = 70 units (`optimisation-backlog.md:785`)
  that is **362 units idle and 680 units during the resumed prefill**, against the item's 1.5 GiB = 1,100 units and
  E-K3's 1.55 GiB pool gap. The pool gap also carries the 27 MiB buffer and the other groups, so 0.5-1.0 GiB is the
  part holes explain; the item's 1.5 GiB was an upper bound, not a measurement of the holes.
- At `resume` the expert slab was at 0 MiB and had given back 3,812 VRAM units while 972 MiB of free blocks sat inside
  the pool. Most of that swing is not the loan but the activation arena growing with the prefill's row count (`lend_mib`
  2,047.2 -> 0.0 includes the arena's part, `core/engine.cpp:469-473`); the loan's own part is the 972 MiB the pool
  could not lend.
- With the host tier on (first run) there is nothing to compact: `held 0`, `holes_mib 0.0-1.4` in every view, all 6,974
  MiB of the batch's KV on host (`on_host` per stored session) and `spare_tokens 291,348`. The item's case is the pool
  keeping the blocks, i.e. the second run.
- The counters do not say how much of the held set is *movable*. The blocks above the holes belong to prefix-cache
  entries and to stored sessions, and only the entries nothing is reading or restoring can move. The follow-up has to
  count that (`relocatable_blocks`) before a compaction can be sized.

So the measurement does call for the compaction: above the item's 256 MiB bar by 2-4x, and worth 0.4-0.7 k units, which
is the shape of the item's claim.

## Why the compaction is not in this change

The remedy the item names — d2d copy the highest cached-only blocks down into the holes and remap — is a change to the
pool's top, not a counter, and every piece of it needs a guard this change would have to invent and validate:

- who may move: prefix-cache entries whose blocks no sequence holds and no restore/mover flight is staging
  (`PrefixCache` has `rebind(key, blocks)` and the `straggler_mark`/`drop_stragglers` in-flight rules,
  `core/mem/prefix.cpp:862-935`), and *not* the stored sessions' blocks, which own their block tables; a wrong answer
  here is silent KV corruption, not a slow step.
- the copies: `core/mem/kv.h:192-200` `BlockCopy` and `take_pending_copies()` (`core/mem/kv.cpp:1480`) are the existing
  hand-off, but the manager issues no device copies, so the engine has to run them on its copy stream between steps, and
  `set_loan`'s "no block held at or above the new top" check (`core/mem/kv.cpp:991-1005`) has to stay true across the
  rebind.
- the order: allocation, copy, remap, release, `held_hist_` update, and the loan recomputed so the slab can grow in the
  same tick — a half-done compaction moves the same bytes and buys nothing.
- and the A/B: `holes_mib` near 0 at `finished`/`resume` on this lane, `slab_mib` above 0 and `expert_vram_units` above
  15,415 at `resume`, `resume_hit_tokens` unchanged (the cache is kept, so this is not a drop), greedy text
  byte-identical.

That is M effort on the block-relocation path with a lane A/B of its own, which is a card, not the tail of a counters
change; the card's rule ("otherwise the note, and close") covers it, and the item's own effort column splits the two (`S
(measure) / M`).

Why not the cheap lever instead: dropping the highest cached entries recovers the same top with no copies, and
`drop_stragglers` already exists — but it only runs with the host tier on (`core/mem/prefix.cpp:877`, `if (loan_goal <=
0 || !tiers_->host_on()) return 0;`), i.e. exactly not in the case measured above, and dropping entries trades cache
hits for VRAM (a lossy lever, `resume_ttft` up), which the item does not ask for and this card must not hide.

## Seen and left alone

- The arbiter moves the loan only when the spare is the current loan plus a whole prefill chunk
  (`core/engine.cpp:2417-2420`), so a spare under `max_tok` tokens never reaches the slab from a loan of 0; with 4-token
  blocks the `finished` spare of 32,972 tokens is 435 MiB, above any chunk, so this did not distort the reading — but a
  group with a coarse block size could hold a few MiB back this way.
- `history.cpp:76-77` reads `kv_bytes_used` and `kv_tokens_used` for the dashboard's history and clamps `kept` to
  `used`; neither sees the holes. Not touched.
