# K-50b / `mla_expand_gate` at prefill — closed, with the numbers

The item (issue #266) asks to give `kv_expand_gate_quant` an MB axis (more than one token tile a
workgroup) and lift `kFuseRows 64`, so that a prefill step above 64 rows keeps the fused quantiser
instead of paying a 33.5 MB round trip and a separate quantiser a layer-step. It was built, it is
byte-identical, and it is **slower**: the fused launch costs 1.39 × to 1.57 × the
`mla_expand_gate` + `had_quant_act_i8g` chain it replaces, at 512 rows as at 64, and at every height
in between. CARDS.md §2's second bar ("the kernel, op or step the item targets is measurably faster
in ms") therefore fails, and the item takes the path it names for that case: closed without code.

The numbers are in `notes/backlog/measurements/K-50b.md` (three interleaved runs a side, one R9700 on
`iks`, kernel lane). Summary, µs a layer-step:

| geometry | chain (before) | fused (after) | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| M=512, heads=64 | 201.00 + 47.52 = **248.52** | **345.79** | 1.39 × |
| M=512, heads=32 | 98.98 + 24.98 = **123.96** | **180.00** | 1.45 × |
| M=256, heads=64 | 111.34 + 25.73 = **137.07** | **192.14** | 1.40 × |
| M=256, heads=32 | 54.15 + 11.95 = **66.10** | **103.69** | 1.57 × |
| M=128, heads=64 | 65.03 + 13.16 = **78.19** | **116.82** | 1.49 × |

| M=64, heads=32 | 21.65 + 5.62 = **27.27** | **38.20** | 1.40 × |

## What was built, and then reverted

`fa811e4a` takes all of it back out; this list is what a re-open would start from.

- **Kernel** (`libr4d/r4d_mla_kv_bf16.hip`): `kvq_mb_body`, a prefill form of the same chain where a
  workgroup takes `kQMB = 4` token tiles of 16 and each wave runs the whole contraction of its
  (column tile, token tile) instead of a quarter of it; the gate product goes to
  `ag[kQMB][16][kQG]` LDS, one `__syncthreads()`, then 32 waves quantise the 64 tokens. Decode heights
  (`M <= 64`) keep the old grid exactly, so the decode rows are untouched byte for byte.
- **Row** (`libr4d/r4d_rows.cpp`): `cMlaExpandGateQuant` said `C_LE("M", 64)` while the kernel had a
  form for `M > 64`, so a prefill-height issue resolved to `<none>`; the bound is now 65535. Without
  this the arch lift would have been a hole, not a speed-up.
- **Arch** (`arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_attn.h`): `kFuseRows` gone, `fused_q = op_expand_quant &&
  abs_rows == T`.
- **Tests**: an emu prefill block in `tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_kv.cpp` (heads 32 and 2 × M 65, 70, 133:
  the fused emission equals the gate-true projection plus `had_quant_act_i8g` bit for bit, and its
  rows equal the same rows a token tile a workgroup), a row-resolution case in `tests/plugin_test.cpp`
  (M 64/70/133/512 resolve the fused row; 70/133/512 did not before the constraint fix), and an arch
  case in `tests/hyv4flash_arch_test.cpp` (the fused expansion takes a step past the old cap).
  Every one of them passed on that tree, and the byte parity held: `rad-kbench` on the after tree read
  240 of 240 checks against `libref` and `libavx`, the base tree's 222 of 222 (the two sides' fixtures
  are the rows under test, not the whole graph), and `tests/r4d_emu/test_mla_kv.cpp`'s prefill block
  holds the fused emission to the chain bit for bit.

## Why close instead of tuning further

The kernel work did what it promised and the fusion still loses:

- The MB form is **37 % faster per row than the one-tile form it extends** (0.675 µs a row at M=512,
  heads=64, against 1.072 µs a row for the one-tile form at M=64), and it is 0.19 µs a row behind the
  chain (0.675 against 0.485). A win needs the fused launch at or below the chain, i.e. another 28 %
  — from a form whose remaining cost is not overhead but work.
- The extra cost is the in-kernel gate-plus-quantiser phase: 2.9–4.1 × the standalone quantiser at
  every height (144.8 µs against 47.5 µs at M=512, heads=64; 30.4 µs against 9.1 µs at M=64; 51.8
  against 13.2 at M=128, heads=64). The traffic the fusion saves is the 16.8 MB product written and
  read back, 33.5 MB a layer-step and ~1.4 GB over a 42-layer chunk, worth ~2.3 ms a chunk at this
  card's 600 GB/s; the slower launch adds 42 × 97.3 µs ≈ 4.1 ms. The chain is already faster than the
  traffic it moves, so the round trip is not what costs.
- Where the fusion pays is the **launch it removes**, not the bandwidth: at decode heights it saves
  one launch a layer-step, which is a few microseconds on this card, and that is K-50 part 1's win. A
  prefill step amortises launches instead.

## Reopen condition

Either a fused form that does not serialise the quantiser behind the chain inside a workgroup (so the
in-kernel quantiser stops costing 2.9–4.1 × the standalone one), or a served-prefill A/B on a TP4 lane
(pf6 / pf20, profile-ops and tok/s) that shows the ~1.4 GB a chunk the fusion removes mattering more
than the ~4.1 ms the slower launch adds. This run had no server lane, so the end-to-end half of the
item's DoD was not measured — the op-level result is what closed it, and it is unambiguous.

## Seen but left alone

The same 1.45 × holds at M=64, where the **base** already fuses (68.30 µs fused against 47.32 µs for
the chain), so K-50 part 1's fusion is worth a look of its own if the engine issues 64-row steps
outside decode: the launch it saves there is a few microseconds, not the 21 µs it costs. Not part of
this item, and not touched.
