## Why it closes without code

- The item's premise is real: a 60K prompt does pay 13-14 recalls, one per other prefill chunk, and they are gone
  with the change (0). The `arr60` phase that shows it stays on the branch; the counter does not, because a card that
  closes merges its note and no code (`CARDS.md` §2).
- The price is the memory. The only way to stop the 100 ms arbiter (`Engine::loan_tick` -> `spare_tokens`) lending
  the reserve straight back is to refuse it the prompt's blocks, so the expert slab goes without them for the whole
  prefill instead of between two recalls: 60,502 tokens at 14.25 KiB (`kv_latent_sparse` 54.00 KiB a 4-token block +
  `kv_index_keys` 3.00 KiB) is ~843 MiB, from 7.13 GiB of KV pool. That is the 2 s: the prefill's chunk goes
  682 -> 750 ms and the decoder it shares the step with 23.8 -> 25.8 ms, i.e. the expert plane misses more while
  the arrival holds its blocks.
- The drains it removes were cheaper than the measurement's own spread: the three "before" TTFTs span 1.08 s
  (18.815-19.892 s), and removing all 13 recalls saved nothing measurable. The item's 5-50 ms a drain would have
  been 65-650 ms; the arm that removes them is 1.96 s slower, so the recall is not where a 60K prefill's time goes.
- The arena half of the Change is already handled and was left alone: `arena_step` has a 2 s floor
  (`arena_tick`), which a chunked prefill keeps refreshing, so the arena loan is recalled once per prefill wave, not
  once a chunk. That is why the counter the item asked for counts the KV loan and not the arena one.

## What would have to be true to win

A reserve shorter than the prompt: pin the next K chunks rather than the whole prompt (K = 1 is the old behaviour,
K = all of it is this branch), which trades the memory back for one recall every K chunks — worth trying only with a
measured per-recall cost, since the 13 recalls of a 60K prompt together are under the TTFT's run-to-run spread.
`RADIANCE_PROFILE_EVERY=1` on a `mem_test`-shaped recall, or a stamp around `Mover::reclaim(kLendKV)`, would give
that number; the item's "5-50 ms" was an estimate that this lane does not confirm.
