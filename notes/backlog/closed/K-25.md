Item as written (`notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`, lines read at HEAD):

> - Change: `r4d_qsa_select_bf16.hip:417-760`: one workgroup of 256 a row does the radix select over the

## 1. The split the item asks for is already there, and predates the audit

At the audit commit `c655f58c` -- and at this branch's merge base `34862f3d` -- `libr4d/r4d_qsa_select_bf16.hip`
already carries the multi-workgroup select the item specifies:

- `constexpr int kParM = 32;` -- the query rows the split path serves (the item's "M <= 48" decode bound);
- `par_chunks(nb) = min(ceil(nb / 4096), 16)` -- the item's own `ceil(G/4096)` chunking, capped at 16;
- `split = M <= kParM && nchunk > 1 && scratch != nullptr && scratch_bytes >= par_scratch_bytes(topk) &&
  chunk <= kParCap && chunk <= lds && nchunk * topk <= lds` -- so a served engine, which passes scratch, takes
  the two-kernel `qsa_local` + `qsa_merge` form (a radix histogram a chunk into scratch, then the merge), and
  the single-workgroup kernel is left for `M > kParM` (a prefill chunk).

T-03 measured exactly that in a served 63K session: `qsa_select` is stamped as `qsa_local_kernel` +
`qsa_merge_kernel`, 24 launches a selecting pass, **382-390 us a pass** (`measurements/T-03.md` section 1,
three runs). Nothing is left to build for the item's Change beyond its second lever.

## 2. The second lever (`masked 1`) was built, proven byte-identical, and measured

The work done under this card and dropped again:

- `libr4d/r4d_qsa_select_bf16.hip`: a `masked` template parameter threaded through both radix bodies, the wide
  body and the split body, so every key operand reads the score alone (`page_or_live<Masked>` -> 0, a live page)
  and no `tb[b]` load remains in the passes that only want a key; `libr4d/r4d_rows.cpp` declares
  `masked` (`RAD_P_INT`, optional) on `qsa_select_i32`; libref and libavx take and validate it (the loader
  refuses differing schemas); `docs/OPS.md` gains its bullet; Hy4-flash's indexer states `masked` 1 on both
  its `qsa_select` rows (its score plane comes from `idx_score`, which writes -inf past a row's live count).
- Byte-identical, both harnesses: `tests/r4d_emu/test_qsa.cpp` -- 323 fixtures, 2911 launches, 0 failures, 0
  branches unreached, masked 1 on a conformed plane equal to masked 0 (and to the in-file reference) in the
  single, wide and split forms, and masked 1 on an unconformant plane selecting differently, so the parameter
  bites; `libr4d/r4d_selftest.cpp --case qsa_select_i32` on the card -- every variant `masked read ok, masked
  same selection ok`; RDNA4 ISA suite PASS, 32 kernels, 0 errors, 0 warnings.
- Measured (`measurements/K-25.md`): at every served decode shape, masked 1 against masked 0 is inside noise --
  16384/65536 x M 1, 8, 32: 13.7-31.4 us against 13.6-31.5 us, agreeing within 3 % once each variant's first
  timed call (which pays the fresh scratch's first touch) is set aside, with no consistent sign. In the
  single-workgroup kernel the lever is 4.6x at 16384 x 64 rows (20.3 us against 93.2) -- a prefill shape, and
  gone again at 65536 x 64 and x 256.

Why it buys nothing where it would matter: the split body prefetches a thread's pages and scores into
`sv` / `pv` register slices before it computes the first key (`r4d_qsa_select_bf16.hip:973-989`, "one round
trip instead of one a key"), so the page loads are already in flight beside the score loads and the 16-64 KB of
block table, L2-resident after the first chunk, costs nothing measurable; only the single-workgroup kernel, one
workgroup a row, has that load in its critical path.

## 3. The item's premise does not hold, so its bar cannot be met

The item's Why says 12 launches a selecting decode step cost 1.8-3.7 ms past 32-64K. Measured as the served
engine runs it, a launch is 14-19 us at 16384-65536 (section 1 of the measurement file), i.e. **0.17-0.23 ms a
step** for 12 launches -- and T-03's stamps found 382-390 us for a whole selecting pass (0.4-0.6 ms a step, as
it wrote). The premise is high by 8-20x against the first and 3-9x against the second, so even a select that
cost nothing would not reach the item's "10-20 % of a c1 decode step": at 69.4 tok/s (14.4 ms a step) the term
is 1.2-4 % of the step, not 12-26 %. This is the same shape of finding as K-45: the audit row predates the
split path's arrival and its per-launch numbers.

The merge bar's first clause is met trivially (nothing changes) but the second is not (no ms gained at the
shapes the item names), so the item's "otherwise" applies: closed with these numbers and this reason, and the
card's change reverted. A re-proposal needs a served shape in which the select is a real term again -- i.e. a
per-launch time at 60K+ in the hundreds of us, from a served stamps run, not from `--perfidx` of the
single-workgroup form.
