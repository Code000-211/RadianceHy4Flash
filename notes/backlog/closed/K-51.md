# K-51 Small-N trunk GEMMs at prefill: measured, closed without a change

**Closed. No kernel change.** The item's premise does not reproduce at this HEAD, and both of the
locations it names were measured: neither can move the number the item is about.

- The small-N IQR prefill GEMM is **1.24-1.35x** below `attn_output`'s rate, not 4x (83.0 us
  against 516.0 us at the item's own Verify pair on one clock state, 84.1 against 498.5 on the
  other; 1.24x against the same-K `attn_output` shape).
- The decode claim at `IqrPre::begin` (the item's "half the blocks of an N tile wait") **is under 1 %
  of the call**: implemented as a half-part claim and measured interleaved against the merge base,
  it is a tie (min 83.0 against 82.1 us), with a hint of a small loss at the median. The whole decode
  - codes to plane, trellis table, plane write - is 6.8 us of 83.0 us; the part of it a smarter claim
  could overlap is a fraction of that.
- A 64-wide N arm (the item's first option) cannot add waves to the shape: q_a's grid is **2 blocks a
  CU = 16 of the 24 resident waves**, entirely resident, and a narrower tile halves the work each
  block owns instead of adding work in flight. The kernel's header records that arm as measured when
  the tile was chosen: "8 x 1 waves (256x64): ties at o and gate_up" (`r4d_gemm_8a8_prefill.h`).

## The item's two locations, accounted for

1. `libr4d/r4d_rows.cpp` `gemm_iqr_tiled` (HEAD 9556-9566): `NOTUNE`, constraints `K % 256`,
   `N % 128`, `group 128`, `dtype i8a8 i8a8h`, and its own doc says what it is - "the call decodes the
   weight's codes into the int8 fragment plane in its scratch, once, and runs gemm_i8a8_tiled over
   it". A tile axis like the w4a8 rows' `tGemmPrefill` (option 3) means a new tunable, its arms and
   their tuning; nothing at this shape rewards it (below).
2. `libr4d/r4d_gemm_iqr.hip` `IqrPre::begin` (HEAD 865-935; the item cites 782-842). The code is as
   the item describes: 8 parts a tile, one claim a part, all eight waves of the claiming block on
   that part, the other blocks spinning on `decoded >= 8`. What it costs was measured, not assumed
   (below).

## What was measured, and how

One card, gfx1201 (Radeon AI PRO R9700), 210 W cap, ROCm 10.1.0, `AMD clang version 24.0.0git`,
full build of this tree (`-DRAD_WITH_HIP=ON -DRAD_GPU_TARGETS=gfx1201`), merge base `cf11ed50`.
The row's own launch at the item's shapes:

    gpu1 build/bin/r4d_selftest <libr4d.so> --perfiqr <N> <K> <M> 5 20

(`iqr k5 tiled` = the u5 trellis form on `gemm_iqr_tiled`; 20 iterations a run; the times below are
minima of 6 interleaved runs, medians in brackets, because this box's cards sit at two clock states
that differ by 8 %.) `rad-kbench --bench` cannot reach this row at these shapes - its recorded
fixtures pick other rows for these N and K, the finding K-06 wrote down - so the row is measured
directly.

### The item's Verify pair

| shape (N, K, M) | time | TMAC/s |
|---|---|---|
| 1024, 3072, 2048 (q_a) | 83.0 us [84.5] | **77.6** |
| 3072, 8192, 2048 (attn_output) | 516.0 us [519.3] | **99.8** |
| 3072, 3072, 2048 (attn_output's K, the item's other half) | 201.2 us | **96.0** |

So the gap is 1.29x against `attn_output` on this clock state (1.35x on the other) and 1.24x against
the same-K shape. The 4x, and the
audit's §7.1 "q_a 0.36 ms a call at 18 TMAC/s" (`B-kernels-prefill.md`), do not reproduce: 0.36 ms
is 4.3x this row's own launch of the same shape. The item's per-call figures (q_a "100 us") are the
ones the kernel matches.

### Where the time goes at q_a's shape

K ladder (N 1024, M 2048), medians of 3: K 512 20.1 us, 1024 33.0, 3072 84.1, 8192 206.5 -
`T = 8.4 us + 0.02424 us * K` (least squares over the four points, +-1 us). The 8.4 us is
K-independent (the C store: 2048x1024 bf16 is 4 MiB in 8.4 us, plus the call's setup); the rest is
the K loop.

M ladder (N 1024, K 3072), one call, medians of 3:

| M | 128 | 512 | 1024 | 2048 | 3072 | 4096 | 8192 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| us | 49.9 | 43.6 | 53.9 | 84.1 | 121.6 | 151.5 | 259.8 |
| TMAC/s | 8.1 | 36.9 | 59.8 | 76.5 | 79.4 | 85.2 | 99.3 |
| waves a CU | 1 | 4 | 8 | 16 | 24 | 32 | 64 |

(M 128 and M 512 leave most of the machine idle - 8 and 32 blocks over 64 CUs - so those two columns
are the waves a *busy* CU holds.)

N ladder (M 2048, K 3072): N 1024 84.1 (76.5), 1536 129.6 (74.5), 2048 146.4 (88.0), 2560 179.3
(89.8), 3072 201.2 (96.0) TMAC/s.

The rate follows the work one call leaves resident, not the tile. `libr4d/isa/res.sh` on the built
kernel: `iqr_prefill_kernel` 240 VGPR, 39-44 SGPR, 19460 B LDS, 6 waves a SIMD - 24 waves a CU (3
blocks of 8). A block is 128x128 = 8 waves of 32x64, so waves a CU = M*N/131072: q_a has 16 of the
24 slots and `attn_output` 48 (two rounds). A narrower N arm halves the work a block owns and
doubles the blocks, so the same 16 waves: no slot is added, and the A fragment is staged twice as
often. That is why the header's 256x64 arm ties rather than wins, and why its 12/16-wave variants
("192 or 256 activation rows a block") tie to 1 % slower: they all trade away the same 128
accumulators a wave.

### The claim at `IqrPre::begin`, measured

Implemented: a claim is half a part along K (16 claims an N tile instead of 8), so at the served
chunk's 16 M bands every block decodes half a part instead of eight blocks decoding and eight
waiting. Built as a full `libr4d`, measured interleaved against the merge base on the same card,
6 rounds each:

| shape | base min [median] | half-part claim min [median] |
|---|---|---|
| 1024, 3072, 2048 | 83.0 [84.5] | 82.1 [87.9] |
| 3072, 8192, 2048 | 516.0 [519.3] | 517.2 [520.2] |

A tie at the minimum and a slight loss at the median: the eight parts **already** decode in parallel
on eight of the sixteen blocks a tile, and what the other eight wait for is under 1 % of the call.
The whole decode costs 6.8 us at q_a - `gemm_iqr_tiled` 83.0 against `gemm_i8a8_tiled` 76.2 at the
same shape (7 runs, 75.6-77.3) - and nothing at all at `attn_output`, where it is 516.0 against
541.7: at large N the 5-bit code read wins back more than the decode costs. That is the ceiling for
the item's option 2 (make the claim global / finer): at most the 6.8 us, i.e. 8 % of one call, and
only if the decode were removed rather than shared - and the sharing is what was just measured to be
worth nothing. The code was reverted; this paragraph is the record.

## The Verify's open question, answered

"Resolve which row serves `kv_a` (N 576) and `index_head` (N 16) above 64 rows": neither reaches the
IQR tile. `RADIANCE_HOST_DEVICES=2 build/bin/radiance` on the real container, from a nospec config
with `kernels: libr4d` and `debug-graph: true` added, one 6680-token prompt:

| op | row |
|---|---|
| `attn_q_a` N 1024 K 3072 | `gemm_iqr_tiled` (M > 64), `gemm_iqr_nt_m64` (M <= 64) |
| `attn_kv_a` N 576 K 3072 | `gemm_bf16_nt_tiled` (the bf16 `gemm_nt` row) |
| `index_head` N 16 K 3072 | `gemm_bf16_nt_m64` |
| `attn_output` N 3072 K 4096 | `gemm_i8a8_tiled`; N 3072 K 6912 likewise |
| N 3072 K 1024, N 1024 K 1024, N 4096 K 3072 | `gemm_iqr_tiled` |

The startup's per-op table (`profile-ops`, rank 0) lists the same keys: `gemm_nt_q N=1024 K=3072
#7 blk.0.attn_q_a.weight` and `gemm_nt N=576 K=3072 dtype=bf16 #12 blk.0.attn_kv_a.weight`. So the
item's "both prefill tiles need N % 128" holds for both - and neither of those two weights is a
candidate for a narrower IQR tile, because the bf16 row serves them at every M.

## What a served A/B would have added, and why there is none here

The item's merge bar wants the served row as well (stamps / `profile-ops`). Both were attempted on
this host and neither produced a usable number; saying so is better than quoting one:

- `stamps` (`RADIANCE_AQL_STAMP=1`, a `gpu2` server on the `pf6` phase): 195592 rows written, all
  on queue 2050, over the 138 s of model load and warm-up, and **none** inside the probe's window
  (the phase's own dispatches are not stamped). Every row has the same `records=1071` and the
  warm-up's kernel mix, so the file describes the load, not the prefill.
- `profile-ops` (`profile-ops: true`, `RADIANCE_PROFILE_EVERY=1 RADIANCE_LOG_STEPS=1`): the table is
  there (rank 0, log line 315), but it syncs every op - `ms_step` 90.7 against 6.2 unprofiled, 15x -
  and its per-op totals mix the run's first (68-token) request with the measured one, so its
  absolute times are not per-op times. It is what the audit's 0.36 ms a call most likely came from.
- The backlog lane itself cannot start on this host: `scripts/backlog/lanes.sh`'s local line sets
  `ngram-placement: ram`, and the container needs 116 GiB for that table against 102-104 GiB
  available here. The served attempts above used the same config with `ngram-placement: auto`.

The chunk geometry those runs confirmed, for the record: a 6680-token prompt is prefilled as
1984 + 1984 + 1984 + 720 + 8 (steps 96-100 of the run log) - 16 M bands, the geometry the claim
measurement above uses.
