# D-13 (expert-union- and residency-aware speculative verify depth, SpecFit): CLOSED -- no pre-step signal: a per-row routing oracle saves 0.7 % of a lane A c8 step at equal tokens, the best pre-step predictor 0.1 %

**The item as written** (backlog D-13, issue #303):
- **Today:** price each verify row by the expert union it pulls in, or by a per-token/prefix cold-miss estimator from recent per-sequence residency, instead of the flat `load_row_ms[nn]` plus tile jumps (`core/sched/draft_depth.h:247-254, 315-364`).
- **The aim:** pick per-sequence depths that maximise `E[committed] / predicted_step_ms`.
- **The constraint:** selection reads draft-side ancestors and pre-step cache state only.
- **Why:** at TP2 DFly2 c8 the misses cost 7.3 ms a step on u3 and about 32–36 ms a step on u4 (#288, `measurements/issue-147.md` §4).

**What this closes on.** The question that decides the item comes before any design: before a verify runs, can anything predict which draft rows will bring in extra misses? Target routing is known only once the verify has run.

We measured it, per row, on both TP2 lanes.
- **Ceiling:** an oracle that knows every row's routing can save little at equal committed tokens:
  - lane A (u4, disk Engram): +0.7 % at c8, +1.6 % at c4;
  - lane B (u3, RAM): +0.6 % at c8, +0.2 % at c4.
- **Predictor:** what pre-step state predicts captures almost none of it: +0.1 % at lane A c8, and ≤ 0.1 % on lane B.
- **No code is proposed.** Nothing at that size can be told apart from these lanes' run-to-run spread (about ±5 % at c4 in #295; identical code ran 242–365 tok/s at c8 in D-11).

## How it was measured

- **Machine:** adamczhang's, 2026-11-26.
  - 4 × AMD Radeon AI PRO R9700 (gfx1201, 32 GB); every run is TP2 on cards 0,1 (`HIP_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,1`).
  - 270 W cap on every card.
  - ROCm core 10.1 (stock clang, VOPD repeatable off), no container.
  - Link: card 4's AER 0 fatal, 0 nonfatal, 0 correctable; Gen5 x4, before and after both runs.
- **Tree:** `8faf751e` (with #295's local-level price fit), plus a local-only diagnostic that is not in this PR, `RADIANCE_DEBUG_ROWMISS=FILE`:
  - **Card side:** at `Ctx::route_report_impl`, each trunk pass (`draft_pass == 0`, first report of the step) copies the layer's `RadRouting::expert_ids` (`[n_tok][top_k]`, top_k 16) to a per-layer card row. `route_flush` copies those rows to a pinned host bank beside the histogram bank.
  - **Host side:** in `Engine::tiering_dispatch`, where the histogram is credited (rank 0), one line per (step, layer): `R step layer n_tok routed | row:e,e;row:e`. It lists each row's routed experts whose unit the heat engine has off the card. That is the miss definition of `HeatStats::routed_resident` and of `rtrace`'s `live_missed`.
  - **Joined with** `RADIANCE_DEBUG_CONC_LOG` S lines: the verify's entries (sequence, drafts verified, accepted), the step clock, and each DFly2 block's per-position top mass from the step before.
- **Lanes:** #295's configs.
  - **Lane A:** `coupled03.yaml` with the u4 container and `ngram-placement: disk`.
  - **Lane B:** u3, RAM, `host-pool-mib: 23000`.
  - Both run `draft-depth-load: on` with `draft-depth-load-from: 1`.
  - One server per lane, probe `c1,c4,c4,c4,c8,c8,c8,c8` (`scripts/backlog/probe.py`).
- **Data:** 731 and 743 pure decode steps joined (row count checked against `n_tok` every step).
  - Lane A c8: 555 misses a step, in line with the item's 490–540.
  - Lane B c8: 99 misses a step (item: 111).
- **Replay** (offline, by sequence count):
  - **Time model:** step ms ~ base + row·R + tiles + c·U, with U the step's distinct missed (layer, expert) pairs.
  - **Acceptance:** P(draft j accepted | j−1 accepted) by (j, conf(j) bin), taken from the run.
  - **Cutting:** each policy shortens a step's verify only. It cuts a sequence's deepest draft (drafts 1–2 stay, as `load_min`) while `P(acc >= d) < lam (row + c · price)`.
  - **The prices:**

    | policy | price |
    |---|---|
    | `flat` | the mean marginal of a tail row: one number for every row, all the rule can express today |
    | `oracle` | the row's true marginal misses given every other kept row (its routing known: the ceiling) |
    | `seq-oracle` | the true mean marginal of that sequence's tail rows in this step (the ceiling of any per-sequence estimator) |
    | `predictor` | a least-squares fit on pre-step features: that sequence's own misses a row in its previous verify, their EMA, the draft position and conf(d) |

  - **Realised:** tokens are min(acc, d') + 1. The ms drop by the rows, tiles and distinct misses removed. Each policy is swept over lam, and its ms is read at `flat`'s committed tokens.

## Why there is so little

- **A tail row's misses are mostly its own.** Its marginal, given every other row, is 70 % of its own misses (lane A c8: 13.1 of 18.8). So the union is near additive, and a uniform price already carries the mean.
- **The spread moves few decisions.**
  - A row costs about 1.16 ms of trunk plus 1.70 ms of misses at lane A c8 (lam 0.22 tok/ms), so it breaks even at P(acc) ≈ 0.63.
  - The marginal's sd of 0.93 ms moves that point by about ±0.2, and only rows near it change.
  - What the oracle gains is second order: 11.5 more misses saved a step than `flat`, against the tokens those cuts lose.
- **Pre-step state does not carry the routing.**
  - The host has only per-layer histograms summed over a step's rows; per-row routing never leaves the card. A per-row estimator would need a readback like the diagnostic's.
  - DFly2 exposes one top mass per block position, with no router logits or expert guesses.
  - A sequence's misses on one step are promoted or not routed again on the next, so its history predicts its next block weakly.
- **The replay only shortens.** Verifying a cheap row deeper is the mirror case, bounded by the same price spread. That puts the ceiling at about twice the table's at most, still about 1.5 % at lane A c8 with routing known, and pre-step state reaches a small part of it.
- **Consistent with D-11:** a price that moves no decision the step makes moves no throughput.

## Left open (not this item)

- **A uniform-price lead:** on lane A c8 the replayed `flat` rule, with the run's own acceptance and a fitted row price, beats the run as served by 2.9 %, cutting 6.5 rows a step.
- **That is a uniform calibration question** in #295's territory (the online row price at c8 on a high-miss lane), not per-row residency.
- **It is only a lead.** It rests on the lane A c8 time model, whose residual sd is 29.6 ms.
