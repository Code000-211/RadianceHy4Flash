# P-07 — CLOSED: the junction form is 16 dispatches cheaper and 25 us a layer slower

Closed without merging. The junction form is built, tested and lossless, and its own measurement
(`notes/backlog/measurements/P-07.md`) shows it costs more than the launches it replaces: CARDS.md
§2 item 2 (the targeted op measurably faster) fails, and the served decode step is 0.7–1.7 % slower,
so item 3 fails too. What it would take to make it pay is a kernel change of its own, not a
junction form: see the end.

## The item as written

> #### P-07 Junction form for the Engram layers (they fall back to `ihc_pre` + `rmsnorm` + own quantiser)
> - Area kernel fusion+plugin · BI · Gain 0.3-0.5 % / 0.2 % / 0.3 % / 0 · Conf medium · Effort M-L

>   q_a quantises itself (`hyv4flash_attn.h:219-223`). A junction form applying `engram_gate` between
>   `ihc_post` and `ihc_pre`.
> - Why: M: Engram layers 386-417 us against 287; 4 extra floor launches each beside the two GEMMs (K-27).

## What was built

The branch (`440d6e3e`, whose review fixes are `e95f2509` and `ad7389f5`) has the junction form
end to end: the Engram gate as a row in `libr4d/r4d_engram_gate.h`, the `EG` variants of
`ihc_junction_kernel` and `ar_ihc_junction_{2,4}rank_kernel`, the libref and libavx reference
paths, the plugin side that declares the junction with `eg` set when the layer in front of it has
an Engram, the pipelined prefill path's `eg_pre` on lane 0 beside the junction on lane 1, the
`rad_buf_concurrent` naming of `eg.key`/`eg.value`/`eg.mask` (the review's blocking finding), and
`engram_gate` calling the same `r4d_eg::row` so the fused and standalone forms cannot drift.

It works and it is lossless: the four greedy texts are byte-identical between the merge base and
this branch in every run, and the step runs 16–20 dispatches fewer.

## Why it is closed anyway

Decode, one client, TP2 on the `local` lane, medians over four before and three after runs (all
rows, run by run, in `notes/backlog/measurements/P-07.md`):

| | before (`a4c3fd7e`) | after (`ad7389f5`) | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Engram layers' windows (L10, L20, L30; layer 0's window has no left edge) | 403.7 / 376.0 / 401.2 us | 429.3 / 400.4 / 427.7 us | **+25 us a layer** |
| plain neighbouring layers (L11, L31), the control | 278.2 / 278.8 us | 278.5 / 279.0 us | +0.3 us |
| the op: 3 launches (5.9 + 18.0 + 3.4) against 1 fused launch | 27.3 us | 52.4 us | **+25.1 us** |
| served c1 step | 13.79 ms | 14.03 ms | +0.8 % to +1.7 % |
| prefill, 7947 tokens | 2.038 s | 2.041 s | unchanged |

The fused launch is 52.4 us where the tail-0 junction, `engram_gate` and `ihc_pre` together are
27.3 us: the gate's row, moved into the junction, runs at the junction's geometry and costs ~43 us
there against 18.0 us in its own kernel (4 waves, its own LDS parts and block reduce). The ISA
suite says the same from the other side: every Engram variant of `ihc_junction_kernel` spills
`0 VGPR / 21 SGPR` to 68 bytes of scratch a lane, and the Engram `ar_ihc_junction_{2,4}rank`
variants spill 13–120 SGPRs. So the launches the item wanted gone are gone — 16–20 dispatches a
step, twelve of them the ones this form takes over — and the step pays 8 us for every launch it
saves.

## What would make it pay, if it is picked up again

- Give the fused form the gate's own geometry: the gate needs 4 waves over the row and a partial
  sum in LDS; the junction needs its iHC/all-reduce wave map. Either run the gate's row in one of
  the junction's phases behind a single named wave set (the way the tail is), or keep the gate out
  of the junction and instead drop the `rmsnorm`, `quant_act_fp8` and `ihc_pre` launches around it
  into the gate's own kernel (that is a smaller fusion: 3 launches, ~9 us of work).
- Clear the SGPR spills the ISA suite reports for the Engram junction variants before measuring
  again; they are the likeliest half of the 43 us.
- Then the bar is: the Engram layer's window below 403 us and the served step not above 13.79 ms.
