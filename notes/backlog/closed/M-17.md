# M-17 (promote a unit that is in the stager ring VRAM→VRAM instead of h2d again): CLOSED -- in the four decode steps after a prompt none of 576 promotions (and 7 to 9 of 936 fills, 12-15 MiB) has its unit still in the ring; behind a staged pass it would be 2 a step (0.03 % of the step's 9.2 GiB h2d)

Item as written (backlog M-17, issue #128): `engine.cpp:1041` does no swaps behind a staged pass, on purpose. When
`heat.step` would promote a unit currently in the ring, copy it from its ring place before it is lapped.
Gain "prefill-phase h2d 64 MB a step a rank → d2d", confidence medium-low.

## Why nothing reaches it

- **Prefill steps promote nothing.** `tiering_dispatch` (`core/engine.cpp:1058-1063` at `0c2f91db`) calls
  `heat.step` only when the stager did not arm the pass. A prefill step's h2d is the stager's own copies, and
  those are the ring's source. d2d cannot replace them.
- **A swap does not shrink the staged bytes.** A swap keeps the resident count, so lifting the ban would not
  shrink them either.
- **The ring is two layers' share and is lapped every ~2 layers.** At a staged pass's end it holds the last
  layers, or, mid-prompt, the next pass's first `kLead` layers. After the prompt's last chunk those bytes last
  only until `arena_tick` lends the arena top to the slab, 2 s after the last full-plan step. On the measured run,
  that is the third dispatch after the 62K prompt: 936 fills, 1.7 GiB of loan.
- **TP4/u4 never stages.** The whole expert plane is resident ("experts 17.65 GiB … the whole expert plane is
  resident, so more would do nothing"), so the item only exists at TP2-style splits.

## Measurement (sizing, not an A/B)

**Setup.**
- Base `0c2f91db` plus a throwaway probe: one log line per rank per dispatch with the promotions `heat.step`
  proposes. Behind a staged pass it is called and every proposal abandoned, so it shows what that step *would*
  propose.
- The probe also logs how many of those units sit in an unlapped ring place, the fills and the fills in the ring,
  the arena loan, and cumulative h2d and staged bytes.

**Run.**
- TP2 on cards 0,1 (2 × R9700 gfx1201, 270 W cap), ROCm core 10.1.
- `nospec.yaml` with u4, `placement: expert_tiered`, `host-pool-mib: 23000`.
- Stager live: a 557.97 MiB ring, 41 routed layers, experts 23.98 GiB.
- `probe.py` phases `c1,c8,pf20,pf8,lctx1`: a 24K prompt, eight ~7K prompts at once, and a 62.6K prompt then 64
  decode tokens. 2026-11-25, one run.

**Results.** A unit is 1.71 MiB at TP2.

**Prefill h2d.**
- A staged step's h2d is the stager's: median 9,383 MiB a rank. That is 345 ms of link at 28.5 GB/s in an
  886 ms step, so h2d time does matter to the step.
- The d2d-able part is ~2 units a step under the most generous reading: **0.03 %**.
- After the 62K prompt's last chunk, none of the 64 promotions at each of the next two dispatches had its unit
  in the ring, which held the last two layers' share. At the third dispatch the arena loan had taken the top.

## What the 64 MB a step really is

The item's "prefill-phase h2d" matches the **post-prefill catch-up**:
- After the 62.6K prompt, decode ran at the full 64 promotions a step (~102 MiB h2d a rank a step) for ~36
  steps.
- Those steps took 22-56 ms. Once residency settled, at 5-16 promotions, they took 15-17 ms.
- That cost cannot come from the ring: those units passed through the ring layers earlier and were lapped.

Serving it would take a different change: lift the swap ban behind a staged pass, and copy each proposed unit d2d
from its layer's ring place while that layer passes through, as part of the stager's per-layer staging. That
changes the residency policy during prefill. `HeatParams::credit_cap` notes that prefill routing is a poor guide to
decode. It needs its own item and an A/B on the post-prompt decode steps, not this one.
