# K-53 (SwiGLU in the routed gate_up prefill epilogue): CLOSED — the fused epilogue costs more than the quantiser it removes (770 us against 474 us a layer)

Item as written (backlog K-53): `rad_block_moe_fp8.h:1964-1979` runs `moe_gemm_q` -> `w.egu`
[T*16, 2F] -> `swiglu_clamped_had_quant_i8g`; a tile should own 128 gate columns and the matching 128
up columns (`parts 2`) so the swiglu and the quantisation happen in the GEMM's epilogue. Gain
prefill +1.5-2 %, decode -1 launch a layer, confidence medium, effort L. Why: 41 x 254 us = 10.4 ms
(1.8 %) in the quantising kernel and 32768 x 1536 x 2 B = 100 MB written by the GEMM and read back
per layer. Verify: fusion oracle, kbench prefill rows, both class tables.

## What was built and what it did

The row `moe_gemm_q_gated` (and `_mod4`, four classes) runs the routed IQR gate_up GEMM and, in the
same launch, the clamped swiglu of the two parts, the 128-column group's rotation and its `i8` codes
with an `f32` scale a group. It is **byte-identical** to the pair's two launches at every shape the
selftest's gated cases put through the two-class table: the codes and the scales are compared element
by element, no tolerance, and at `03afc0ae` no code or scale differs in any of three runs -- the
prefill shape, a checkpoint's split step (`split 9`, the case that failed on this change's first form
because the fused row walked the run map while its pair walked the packed one), the dot-path step,
both clamps. (One red line did show: run 1's "not slower than a fifth" timing bound on a 28 us
four-class case, 39.2 us against the pair's 30.1, whose quantise read 5.9 us there against 4.1
everywhere else; runs 2 and 3 read 31.4 against 28.5. That is a cold-launch flake, not a result.)

The work to get there, in the branch's commits:

- the fused row got its pair's map, packed map included, so a split cuts the run in two exactly as the
  pair's map does -- that is what makes the split step match by construction rather than by a special
  case;
- the step's `split` operand survives the unfused path again (index 8 is the pair's plane, 9 the fused
  form's scales, 9 or 10 the split), a bug the first form introduced;
- the fused row is declared outside `r4d_moe_decode_band`'s heights only, so decode keeps the tuned pair;
- the gate tile's product moved from a `gpk[NT]` register per tile into the shared group, which took
  the gated kernels from 18-56 VGPR spills a lane at eight token tiles to 3 or none, and the group's
  line is padded by 8 bf16 so a wave's sixteen rows do not all land on one bank slot.

And it came out **slower than the pair it replaces**, at the item's own shape and at decode.

One defect stays named here rather than fixed: at `03afc0ae` the four-class branch issued its fused form
with 10 operands where its row reads up to index 14 (`[12]` codes, `[13]` scales, `[14]` split), so the
four-class fused row either resolved with null trailing operands or fell back to the pair -- the
selftest's four-class rows cannot tell, since they compare against the pair either way. The two-class
form, the one the served model uses, had the right count. It is moot with the revert, and it is one more
reason this shape is not worth fixing forward: `measurements/K-53.md` §2 carries the detail.

## Why it does not merge: CARDS.md §2's second clause fails at the item's own shape

- **At the item's prefill shape the fused row is 7 to 8 % behind.** M 2048 x F 1536 x K 1536, top_k 16,
  128 experts, three-bit, limit 10 -- 32768 routed rows, the model's prefill at 2048 tokens a rank, one
  card, three runs (`measurements/K-53.md` §1): the pair 3644.0 / 3652.4 / 3671.0 us (GEMM 3172.6 /
  3189.3 / 3197.8 + quantise 471.4 / 463.1 / 473.2) against the fused row 3948.2 / 3946.7 / 3942.0 us,
  i.e. **+274 to +298 us a layer**. Over 41 layers that is +11 to +12 ms on a 16384-token chunk where
  the item expected -10.4 ms.
- **The epilogue is what costs, and it has nothing to hide behind.** Its swiglu, its staged group, its
  rotation and its code stores are about 770 us against the standalone quantiser's 474 us for the same
  32768 rows -- and the standalone quantiser has the whole grid for it, while the fused block does the
  same work for the 128 rows it holds, one 128-column group at a time, and cannot take more rows a
  block without giving up the eight-token-tile packed walk that makes its GEMM fast. Ablations
  (timings only, results unchecked): replacing the rotation and the quantise with a copy of the staged
  row leaves +3.8 % (3782.4 us against 3645.5), dropping the gate tile's shared-group round trip
  leaves +2.2 % (3720.1 against 3638.5) -- so the rotation is about 170 us, the round trip about 60 us,
  and the swiglu, the staging and the stores the rest.
- **The premise was overtaken by the base.** At this change's first form the pair took the run map at
  four token tiles: 4524.0 us at this shape (on `iks`), and the fused row 4347.8 us, -3.9 %. The base
  then gave the pair the three-bit packed map (another card's change): 3168 us of GEMM at this shape
  instead of 4006, because eight token tiles a block decode a weight unit once for twice the rows. The
  fused row has to walk its pair's map to stay byte-identical, so it cannot keep the cheaper form, and
  the 474 us the fusion removes no longer pays for the 770 us it adds.
- **The item's decode gain is not delivered either, and the fused row is slower there.** The issue
  counts "decode -1 launch a layer"; the fused row measured 9 to 10 % behind the pair at decode heights
  (31.3 us against 28.6 at M 34 x F 512, top_k 2, 8 experts; `measurements/K-53.md` §2), which is why
  it is declared outside the decode band and decode keeps both launches.
- **There is no served row and no lane A/B to fall back on.** kbench cannot lay out Hy4-flash's gate_up
  table for the fused row (`moe_gemm_iqr_gated cannot lay out the table 'w' from
  trellis:codes=u3[1x1] ...: container format error`, `tools/rad_kbench.cpp:3134`), so all 56 of its
  kbench cases are skipped and the plugin falls back to the pair on the served checkpoint. The item's
  Verify step "kbench prefill rows" therefore has no row on this model, and a served A/B would compare
  the pair with itself.

The item's "otherwise" therefore applies: closed with these numbers and this reason, and the branch's
K-53 commits (`be3892eb`, `b4a41096`, `30f91be9`, `4bd1526d`, `f62133ee`, `a024e18e`, `4191a360`,
`46d83871`, `fb2262af`, `27be2a8b`, `2db396cb`, `6e68505c`, `03afc0ae`) are reverted here, every file
they touched back to the merge base's. The branch's unrelated `a1c74325` (a dead index in the
trunk-selection test, which the first review flagged as outside K-53) goes with it, since that file
returns to the merge base's too.
`notes/backlog/measurements/K-53.md` stays as the record of the raw lines those numbers come from.

## What a re-proposal would need (a new item, not a retry of this one)

- An epilogue that beats the standalone quantiser (474 us) with the whole group's columns in one
  block. One 128-column group over the 128 rows a block holds is the shape that lost here, so that
  means either a rotation whose rows are interleaved inside a wave (two rows a wave's worth of
  independent chains, which needs its own function next to `q8_had_group_i8g`) or a block that keeps
  fewer rows than the packed map's entry it walks -- four token tiles a block over an eight-tile entry,
  so the epilogue's rows a wave halve while the decode stays amortised (a fused form of about 3730 us
  by these numbers: still behind 3644).
- Or the same fusion in the *router's* projection, where the GEMM is longer per row and the epilogue
  would be a smaller share.
- And the gate_up table's layout for the `u3` codes has to lay out for the fused row (the kbench
  error above is its own item) before any served e2e claim is possible.
