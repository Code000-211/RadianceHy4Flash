# K-46 (junction arithmetic at prefill: share the `fn` reads across a block's rows): CLOSED — at TP4 the grouped rows cost occupancy as written, and bounded to keep it they measure inside noise

Item as written (backlog §K-46): `R4D_AR_MAX_BLOCKS 512` leaves a block 4 rows at 2048, walked one by one, each
re-reading the 384 KiB f32 `fn`; process a block's rows in groups of 4 with `fn` loaded once a piece into
`acc[4][R+1]`, the barriers of steps 1 and 3 shared, "also the TP1 plain row and the 4-rank twin". Gain prefill
+2-2.5 %, from 84 x 741 us = 62.3 ms a chunk, est. -12..-16 ms. Verify: `tests/r4d_emu`, kbench
`rmsnorm_ihc_junction`, TP2 vs TP1. It was built twice by an outside contributor and measured on their own four-card
box, not on the TP2/u3 lane: the item as written (`577dd089`) and a bounded rework (`d6dfce82`), both on base
`581b014b`. Neither is in this tree and neither is pushed; the commits are available on request. The first loses at
both group sizes it was built at, the second is neutral, so on that box the bar's second clause fails for both and the
third for the first.

## The setup

4 x Radeon AI PRO R9700 (gfx1201), 270 W cap on every card (read before each session, unchanged at the end), TP4,
`hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u4.rad`, `RADIANCE_HY4_PIPE=1`, no speculation, `max-num-batched-tokens 8192`,
`checkpoint-interval 4096`, `prefill-span 2`, `max-model-len 65536`, `max-num-seqs 8`.
The profile pass is the same file plus `profile-ops: true`. One server at a time, each stopped at the end of its
session (`server-exit=0` every time). Before is an unchanged build of `581b014b` in both rounds.

- Round 1 (`577dd089`, the item as written): before, g4 (groups of 4) and g2 (the same commit built with
  `-DR4D_JUNC_GROUP=2`); two e2e sessions each, order before, g4, g2, g2, g4, before, three probe reps a session
  (n = 6); one profile-ops session each.
- Round 2 (`d6dfce82`, the rework): e2e sessions ABBAAB, three reps each (n = 9); two profile-ops sessions a tree
  (before, after, then after, before).

## What was built

- **`577dd089`:** `junction_rows<HC, POST>` takes a block's rows in groups (4 at hc 2 and 4, 2 at hc 8, 1 at odd hc,
  capped so the group's `ybuf`s fit in 48 KiB), loads each `fn` float4 pair once a piece into every row's accumulators
  and shares the barriers of steps 1 and 3; `finish` split into `finish_part` and `finish_gates`. In the TP1 plain
  kernel (from 1024 rows, at least 512 blocks) and in `arij_rows` of both all-reduce twins.
- **`d6dfce82`:** the twins, `r4d_fuse.cpp`, the one-row body and `finish` back to upstream's text. A separate
  `ihc_junction_group_kernel<HC, POST, ADD, CH, G>` with G = 2 (tail on; hc 2, 4 and 8; n <= 12288; M >= 2048 rows,
  `kJuncGroupMinRows`), under `amdgpu_waves_per_eu` set to the one-row kernel's own occupancy; 24 instances. Compiled
  device-only for gfx1201 against base, the other 134 TP1 and 108 twin kernels have the same VGPRs, SGPRs, occupancy,
  spills, scratch and LDS and identical instruction text (0 of 134 and 0 of 108 differ), so decode, verify and short
  prefill run upstream's machine code.

## Parity

Greedy, T 0, logprobs top 5: prefills of 2665, 3600 and 12548 rows then 128 decode tokens (L8k, L12k, L40k), and four
short prompts one at a time (Q0-Q3, 96 tokens). Byte-identical before against g4 and g2 in round 1, and in round 2
7 of 7 in each of the 9 before x after session pairs and the 3 before x before pairs. The same four short prompts sent
at once (S0-S3) are not an A/B: the scheduler groups them by arrival (1+3 in one session, 2+2 in another) and the same

## Why it closes without code

- **Against the bar, at TP4/u4.** (1) holds for both versions. (2) fails for both: round 1 is slower in ms at both
  group sizes, in prefill and in decode; the rework's -4.6 us a call (~0.4 ms over an 84-call chunk) sits inside
  before's own spread (537.2 and 579.5 us in its two round-2 sessions, 7.9 %). (3) fails for round 1 (g4
  -1.9..-2.3 % prefill, both sizes -2.5..-2.7 % `c1` decode) and holds for the rework. The -12..-16 ms a chunk does
  not appear; before's call is 537-580 us on this box, not 741.
- **Why the item as written loses: registers.** Grouping 4 rows in the same template instance as the one-row path
  took the hc 4 kernels from 118 to 255 VGPRs (TP1, one VGPR spilled in the chained form) and from 140 to 256 (4-rank
  twin), 12 and 10 waves a SIMD down to 5, on every launch including decode. Under a register bound held at the
  one-row kernel's occupancy, groups of 3 and 4 spill instead (62-93 VGPRs at hc 4 with post, up to 304 B of scratch a
  lane), so a pair is the only group that fits, and the rework is that pair.
- **Why the twin was the wrong place at TP4.** The graph fuses the 4-rank twin only up to `kjRows4` = 8 rows
  (`junction_rows`, arch/hyv4flash/hyv4flash_common.h:590-596). A long prefill goes through the add, the standalone
  all-reduce and the plain kernel, so grouping inside the twin cannot reach TP4 prefill and could only cost decode,
  which is where round 1's -2.5 % came from (decode `ar_rmsnorm_ihc_junction` ~84 calls a step, ~11 % of the step).

## TP2/u3, which this did not measure

The item's lane is TP2/u3 at 210 W, and world 2 fuses the twin at every height (`junction_rows` returns `max_tok`), so
there the prefill junction is the 2-rank twin. The rework leaves that twin as upstream, so on TP2 it would most likely
not touch the prefill junction at all and is not worth a TP2 run as it stands. A TP2 test of K-46 needs pairs inside
the 2-rank twin under the same register bound; round 1 grouped there without one (2-rank hc 4 at 232-256 VGPRs, 5-6
waves a SIMD). That bounded pair kernel in the 2-rank twin was not written.

## Seen and left alone: kbench `rmsnorm_ihc_junction` at hc 4, M=2

On unchanged `581b014b`, `rad-kbench -m hy4-flash-iqr-v4-u4.rad --op rmsnorm_ihc_junction --max-cases 0 --max-tok 2048`
gives 2016 checked, 2012 passed, 4 failed, the same lines in every tree. All four are `n=3072 hc=4 rows=8 tail=1
post_mag=2 group=128 M=2` against a tolerance of 4.0e-3: host `avx_rmsnorm_ihc_junction` rel_l2 4.100e-3 and
4.407e-3, device `rmsnorm_ihc_junction_bf16` 4.151e-3 and 4.343e-3. Host and device agree, so it reads as a tolerance
a little tight for hc 4 at M=2 rather than a kernel bug; not looked into further. The replay without `-m` has no
junction cases (its fixture was recorded from a Qwen container).
