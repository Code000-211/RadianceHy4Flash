# M-02 (2Q probation area for first-miss admissions): CLOSED — 32 %/45 % of the admissions are reused, so they are copies the LFU path makes once anyway; link h2d +9 %/+58 %, DFly2 c8 -3 %

Item as written (backlog M-02): "Admission for many streams (X2): admit on first miss into copy-free
slots, replay first" — `HeatParams`, `HeatEngine::step` / `observe`, `Mover::balance` / `fill` / `drop`:
a 2Q probation area of lent slots, the move budget following the pass width. Measured basis: the
replay oracle's -18 % (no spec) / -29 % (DFly2) of misses = c8 +1-2 %, DFly2 c8 +2-4 %.

The area was built and tested (commits `1253fd47`, `e9058324`, `af9fc527`, `219581ba`, `47b16c57`, seven
heat tests and two mover tests, all passing under `place_test`), then measured on the `iks-A` lane
against this branch's merge base: `notes/backlog/measurements/M-02.md` (before `4744d929`, after
`47b16c57`, 2 x R9700 TP2 at 210 W, three interleaved runs a side, `nospec.yaml` and
`coupled03.yaml`). The code is reverted, not merged.

## The number

- The premise does not hold on this model. A first miss is routed again inside its three-step window
  in **32 %** of the nospec admissions (278 of 869) and **45 %** of the DFly2 ones (2263 of 5011);
  73 % and 57 % expire unused. The LFU path already puts 2288-2820 distinct units a phase on the
  device (`distinct_promoted`), so the area adds work on top of a path that covers the reused misses
  instead of replacing it.
- The admission is a copy. Link h2d a `c8` phase: nospec **9.53 -> 10.40 GB** (+9 %), DFly2
  **11.55 -> 18.28 GB** (+58 %, promotions 3786-3918 -> 6105-6198, readmits 2693-2817 -> 5089-5213).
- The area's slots come out of the swap budget: `starved_pool` 78-129 -> 137-163 (nospec) and
  488-542 -> 3319-3755 (DFly2) a phase, `quarantined` +10 % / +60 %.
- End to end: DFly2 c8 median **406.20 -> 394.20 tok/s (-3.0 %)**; nospec c8 flat (320.95 -> 320.73)
  but **TTFT 69-71 -> 280-281 ms**, because the admissions are taken while the eight c8 requests are
  still prefilling.
- Output parity holds: the four greedy texts hash the same in all twelve runs.

## Why closed

`CARDS.md` §2 needs the targeted quantity faster in ms and the served rate not worse. Here the targeted
step (an admission) is strictly more expensive than what it replaces: it copies a unit that the LFU
path would have copied once at the moment it was actually reused, and half of those copies are thrown
away three steps later. Two of the three rows fail, and the direction is deterministic (every after
run moves more bytes), not a noisy measurement.

## What a reopen would need

"Copy-free slots" is the part that was not possible with the engine as it stands: an admission today is
a h2d of the unit into the lent slot. An admission is only free if the slot already holds the unit's
bytes, which is the item's "replay first": the miss is served by staging the unit into a slab slot and
that staging copy *is* the residency, so the next dispatch of the same unit reads VRAM instead of
staging again. That is a change to the staged/streamed prefill path (`Mover`'s staging and the
`stage_copies` / `link_staged_bytes` counters are all zero on this lane today), not to the heat
ranking, and it should be measured on the same lane with the same three rows: the admission's reuse
share (32 %/45 % here is the ceiling it can pay back), h2d a phase, and the `c8` tok/s and TTFT.
