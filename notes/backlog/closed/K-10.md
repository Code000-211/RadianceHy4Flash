# K-10 — Router GEMM + select + scatter + quant in one launch

**The fusion is closed without code.** Measured on the local server lane, before (`96569ca9`) against after
(`775503c4`, the fused row as the block uses it), three interleaved runs an arm on each of `nospec.yaml` and
`coupled03.yaml`: the three launches the row replaces lose 24 us a step at c1 (630 -> 606 us, -0.6 us a layer)
and nothing at c8, and **the pass is not shorter** — the routed gate_up that follows the router runs 118 us a
step longer, the trunk linears 85 us shorter, and on `coupled03.yaml` the c1 full pass is 0.5 % longer.
Merge bar clause 2 ("the kernel, op or step the item targets is measurably faster in ms") does not hold for the
step, and clause 3 leans the wrong way on DFly2 c1, so `CARDS.md` §2 says to close. The output was byte-identical
throughout (clause 1).

One part of the change did measure and is merged with this note: `gemm_nt_f32`'s tile body split into its sums and
its store, which makes the stored-bf16 eight-row form (17-63 rows) 62-79 % shorter. That is
`../measurements/K-10.md`.

The fusion was built in full and passed every check; it is in this branch's history (`07263cb6`, fixed for the
served shape in `775503c4`) and taken out in `bd1b2b4d`, so a retry can start from it.

## 1. What the lane found first: the row was wired to the wrong input

The first served run of the PR head (`07263cb6` merged with the base) stopped at the first decode step:

```
E kernel router_gemm_sigmoid_scatter_f32 (libr4d, no tunables, device domain) refused op 'router_gemm_sigmoid_scatter'
  at M=1 [band M<=16] geometry {K=1536 n_expert=512 ... hq_group=128 hq_dtype=i8 M=16 chain=1}: shape this kernel does not serve
```

Hy4-flash's router projects the 3072-wide state (`Config::router_k`, `Wire::route`) and its experts run in the
1536-wide latent; the block handed the row the latent as the projection's input beside a `[512, 3072]` weight. The
mini model of `hyv4flash_arch_test` has a latent as wide as its state, so no check without a card saw it.
`775503c4` gives the row the router's own input and makes the latent the folded codes are of a second, optional
input (`hq_x`, `hq_K`), with the selftest oracle, the emulator test and the arch test at that shape. That is the
"after" below.

## 3. Why (a reading of the stamps, not a separate measurement)

The item counted launches: "fusion removes two chained launches (G-3 price: 0.3-0.5 us each) and the logits round
trip". On the lane the boundaries of these three launches are worth about that and no more: 0.6 us a layer at one
row, nothing at eight. The tables show where the rest goes: what the three launches lose, the routed gate_up behind
them gains, and the shared arm's linears issued alongside lose in turn, so the exclusive time moves between
launches that overlap on the queue while the pass's sum stays where it was (and rises 0.5 % with the drafter's six
rows). The probable mechanism is that the one launch holds the queue until its last workgroup has ranked, where the
pair's quantiser and selection overlapped with their neighbours; that was not isolated here. The logits round trip
stays in the fused form too (the ranking workgroup is not the one that computed the tile).

## 4. If someone retries

- The pass, not the row, is what has to be shorter: `agg.py`'s pass exclusive sum and wall on `nospec.yaml` c1 and
  `coupled03.yaml` c1, not `--perfmoefuse`.
- The fold of the codes is where the row's own time comes from (bare, it was level with the pair at 16 rows); the
  cost is the gate_up that waits for them. A form that writes the codes first and lets the rank trail would have to
  be tried against exactly that.
- K-09 (the selection chain itself, 8.7 us of the 12.2) is the larger term and does not depend on this.
