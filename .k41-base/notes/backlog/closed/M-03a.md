# M-03a (stager arms at 1024 rows, the busy-chunk floor's off-by-one): CLOSED — the case does not arise, and where the floor binds the chunks already fill a step the stager arms

Item as written (backlog M-03 (a), `notes/backlog/optimisation-backlog.md`): `min_rows = top_level + 1` = 1025
against the busy-chunk floor `stage_rows / Q * Q` = 1024, so "a held-back 1024-row chunk is staged again"; the change
"(a) round the floor up to 1040 or arm at `n_tok >= top_level`". PL, effort S. Verify: `/stats experts stage_layers`
on a 1024-row step, the resume TTFT, texts identical stager off / on.

## What the code says (at HEAD, after this branch's revert)

- Arming is the ring *and* the row count: `armed_ = want_ != nullptr && enabled() && b->n_tok >= min_rows_`
  (core/place/stager.cpp:121-123). `want_` is the ring, and the ring comes from the step's arena level: the full plan
  (level 0) lends its top, the span's shared level keeps one of its own, and every other level lends none
  (core/engine.cpp:2500-2503). `min_rows_` is `top_level + 1` (core/engine_bringup.cpp:5086-5102, printed at :5104).
- The served configuration (lane `local`, `nospec.yaml`: `max-num-batched-tokens 2048`, `busy-prefill-chunk auto`,
  `checkpoint-interval` 2048, `prefix-resume nearest`) builds the levels {256, 1024} and `arena_span_rows_ = 0`
  (core/engine_bringup.cpp:1737 puts the levels at `R < max_tok`, :1738 clears the span, :1798 sets it, and
  `--prefill-interleave auto` is per-step, not a span). The server says so at startup:
  `activation arena: 256 tokens use 185.82 MiB, 1024 tokens use 705.88 MiB of 1.37 GiB` and
  `prefill staging: a 523.09 MiB ring at the top of the arena; a step of more than 1024 tokens copies the pooled
  experts of 41 routed layers onto the card`. So a step of 1024 rows takes level 1024, gets `ring = nullptr`, and
  cannot arm whatever `min_rows` says. "Arm at `n_tok >= top_level`" alone is therefore not a lever: it needs (b)'s
  ring at the 1024 level first.
- What is left of (a) is the floor: make the held-back chunk bigger than 1024 rows so it takes level 0 (which arms at
  1024 < 1025 anyway). That is the change this branch built, ran on a lane, and reverts.

## Why it closes without code

- The chunk of exactly 1024 rows is real — 18 and 32 of them in the two base 8-prompt runs — but it is never alone in
  a step. `RADIANCE_LOG_STEPS=1` shows two of them filling one step:
  `step 97 prefill n_seq=2 n_tok=2048 [0] slot=0 q=1024 ctx=2048 [1] slot=1 q=1024 ctx=0`, the next step the same
  shifted. A 2048-row step takes the full plan, gets the ring and is staged: `stage_layers` 41 a pass, 1110 over the
  8-prompt phase. There were **0 steps with `n_tok=1024`** in 1,736 logged steps of the five base runs (also none in
  the lone-prefill and stall runs), so the "held-back 1024-row chunk staged again" the item predicts never happened.
  Where the floor binds, the change only hands the interval's rows out one request at a time
  (`q=1792` + `q=256`, then `q=256` + `q=1792`) instead of `q=1024` + `q=1024`: same steps, same rows, same
  `stage_layers` (1110 both), same time — 15.86/15.95 s before, 16.03/16.03 s after (+0.5..+1.1 %, noise), and the
  sum of the eight TTFTs 82.1/80.8 s before against 85.7/83.2 s after, i.e. if anything worse: the second request of
  a pair now waits a step for its 256 rows.
- The merge bar asks for the targeted op measurably faster in ms (`CARDS.md` §2). It is not faster: the same staging,
  the same ms, and no step that used to be unstaged is staged now. The item's own gain ("-100..-170 ms per prompt
  whose last chunk is 129-1024 rows", "a 256-token step is 223 ms") belongs to (b): a lone last chunk or a resumed
  turn is a step of 528 or 256 rows, which the floor never touches. Measured and unchanged: `pf6`'s 528-row last
  chunk stages nothing on either side (`stage_layers` 123 = 3 x 41, the three 2048-row steps), `tail400` 0.199 ->
  0.192 s.
- Texts identical stager on / off is the (b) item's check (a lone small step, staged or not); this change never
  altered a step's numbers, only which request's rows were in it, and it is reverted, so no text comparison is owed
  here. It would have been owed before a merge, which is another reason the code cannot stand on this evidence.

## What a later card needs from (b)

Give the 1024 level a ring right above its buffers (the item's (b): the span level's `arena_low_off` mechanism,
`core/engine.cpp:2500-2503`, `core/engine_bringup.cpp:5257-5260`; 706 + 1 + 523 MiB fits), then a step of 129..1024
rows has a ring to copy into and `min_rows` can come down to ~128. The item's numbers for that are the last chunks
(`M` §2.4: pf6's 537-row tail, 338 ms of op time) and the resumed turns (`M` §2.6: a 256-token prompt 223 ms), not
the floor. The instrument this card leaves behind: `scripts/backlog/probe.py`'s `pf8` phase (8 x 6.7K tokens at
once) and, for the step shapes, `RADIANCE_LOG_STEPS=1`, which prints `step N phase n_seq=.. n_tok=.. [i] slot=q=..`
a step.
