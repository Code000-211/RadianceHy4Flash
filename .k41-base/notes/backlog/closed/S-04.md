# S-04 (interleave EMA fed only at the stager floor): CLOSED — after S-01 the mean is held down by cached chunk steps, not small ones, and where the change does act it costs the arrival's TTFT

Item as written (backlog §3.5, derivation `audit/D-host-engine-sched-server.md`): `scheduler.cpp:3413` feeds
`ema_chunk_s_` from every non-decode step (`:1532-1541`); "today remainder, tail and small resumed-delta steps pull it
down, so fewer decode-only steps follow the big chunks than `prefill-interleave auto:1` intends". The change measured
here feeds the mean only from a step whose prefill rows reach the prefill stager's floor
(`std::max(quantum, stage_rows_/quantum*quantum)`, 1024 rows in the served config). It was built and committed as
`d0fee96e` with unit tests in `tests/sched_test.cpp` (fixtures at `set_stage_rows(1025)`), and it is reverted in this
commit: on the served lane it has no ms gain to show for the bar's second clause, and where it changes the mean it
costs the arrival's TTFT (third clause).

## Why the floor does not fix it

- With `--checkpoint-interval 1984` (S-01) every real chunk is 1984-1985 rows, so the small steps the item names are a
  warmup's 8-9 rows, a 73-token prompt, a 615-row resumed delta and a 720-row prefix hit: a handful a run. They are
  not the cheap ones either — the 8-row steps just before the arrival cost 0.376 s and 0.724 s (0.387 s and 0.739 s in
  the other binary; cold weights), so the floor drops *expensive* samples, and the mean enters the first arrival at
  0.146 where the old code had 0.244.
- The samples that do hold `ema_chunk_s_` down are chunk steps: 1984 rows alternating 0.2314 s and 0.0100 s in the
  `pf6`/`pf20` phases (a 1,984-row pass in 10 ms computes nothing — a warm/cached chunk at `ctx=9920`), seven of
  them in the two prefill phases. A floor on rows keeps them; the mean sits at 0.12-0.16 when the arrival starts,
  against a real chunk step of 0.46-0.57 s, whichever binary runs.

## Where the change does act, it costs the arrival's TTFT

A pristine arrival — phases `stall1,stall4` only, so no cached chunk in the history before it — is where the item's
premise holds: `ema_chunk_s_` 0.296 / 0.392 / 0.450 becomes 0.582 / 0.611 / 0.613, against chunk walls of
0.583 / 0.679 / 0.623, and `interleave_steps()` 19 / 17 / 23 becomes 37 / 31 / 34, so the prefill is held for about a
whole chunk instead of about half (29 decode-only steps between two chunks where there were 13). The arrival's TTFT
then rises 2.975 → 3.265 s at one decoder and 3.088 → 4.012 s at four: +0.29 s and +0.92 s, past the lane's ±0.3 s
spread, and the background client's wall time for its 426 tokens is unchanged (8.64 against 8.63 s). The decoders gain
what the arrival loses, and nothing gains in aggregate.

That trade is exactly what `prefill-interleave auto:1`'s share of 1.00 asks for, so the change is not wrong — it makes
the configured share effective where the mean happens to be set by small steps. But the card's gain ("steadier decoder
cadence under arrivals") is not measurable on the served lane, and the price is on the arrival's TTFT, which the bar's
third clause holds against. Hence the note, and no code.
